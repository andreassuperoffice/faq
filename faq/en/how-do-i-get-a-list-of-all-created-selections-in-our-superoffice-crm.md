# How do I get a list of all created selections in our SuperOffice CRM?

Updated on 28 Dec 2025

I want to know how many selections we have created in SuperOffice CRM. How do I do it?

To get a list of all selections that have been created in your SuperOffice CRM, please follow the steps below:

1. Click on the **Find** button in the toolbar at the bottom of the SuperOffice screen.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6465349-9bjDryp13xGay9x5oYHLXsgb8ctbO7mr91echQTy0CFY9Ey1B1SOY0n8yEutBsDp-0/image-20251228120254-1.png)
2. Then click on **Find Selection**.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6465350-Hwq5uRTGB4AcqgHchy1nBf15GkXckBi6h7WqGiQm7eHeetNO3DwgpU9M9SlcrMMh-0/image-20251228120351-2.png)
3. In the field **Selection name**, type in the % sign, which means that you are searching for all selections, and then click on the **Find** option. This search will give you the list and number of all existing selections.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6465351-R3NzTegPtY4aMOmxq0eWgykGb8BL37Su18hdXWbFaaYJRaX16uIFtgPQtmalDe7q-0/image-20251228120647-3.png)

   **Note!** If you want to get a list of all selections created by a specific user, in addition to**the Selection name** criteria, select **User ID** criteria and choose the user whose selections you want to retrieve.
