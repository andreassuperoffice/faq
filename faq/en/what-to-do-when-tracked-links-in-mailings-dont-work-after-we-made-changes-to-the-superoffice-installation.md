# What to do when tracked links in mailings don’t work after we made changes to the SuperOffice installation?

Updated on 26 Mar 2026

We have changed the domain name of our SuperOffice installation, and after this change, [tracked links](https://docs.superoffice.com/en/marketing/tracked-links/learn/index.html) do not work anymore. Why did it happen, and how do we fix it?

All tracked links ([unsubscribe](https://docs.superoffice.com/en/marketing/editor/learn/add-unsubscribe-link.html) link included) in SuperOffice Mailings contain the hashed string, which is based on the customer center domain.

To ensure that the tracked links, which were created before the SuperOffice installation domain change, work, you can keep the old customer center installation accessible.
