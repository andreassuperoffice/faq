# How do I get an overview of all contacts without any e-mails and/or documents in SuperOffice CRM?

Updated on 15 Jul 2025

How do I get an overview of all contacts without any e-mails and/or documents in SuperOffice CRM?

This is the way you can make a list of contacts without any e-mail and/or documents:

1. [Create a selection that lists all contacts with documents and e-mails](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-get-an-overview-of-all-contacts-without-any-e-mails-andor-documents-in-superoffice-crm/#1)
2. [Create the interest "Have documents and emails" for contacts in SuperOffice CRM](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-get-an-overview-of-all-contacts-without-any-e-mails-andor-documents-in-superoffice-crm/#2)
3. [Use a bulk update to update all the contacts with documents and e-mails](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-get-an-overview-of-all-contacts-without-any-e-mails-andor-documents-in-superoffice-crm/#3)
4. [Create a selection of all contacts in SuperOffice CRM](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-get-an-overview-of-all-contacts-without-any-e-mails-andor-documents-in-superoffice-crm/#4)
5. [Create a selection that lists only contact with the interest](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-get-an-overview-of-all-contacts-without-any-e-mails-andor-documents-in-superoffice-crm/#5)
6. [Create a combined selection with contacts without documents and/or emails](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-get-an-overview-of-all-contacts-without-any-e-mails-andor-documents-in-superoffice-crm/#6)

You must have the [combined selection](https://docs.superoffice.com/en/search-options/selection/learn/combine.html) feature to get an overview of contacts without assigned documents and/or e-mail.

**Note!** Only our Sales and Service Premium and Marketing plans have the combined selections feature available. Please contact your SuperOffice contact if you need this feature.

**Create a selection that lists all contacts with documents and e-mails**

To do so, please follow the steps below:

1. Click on the **Find** (1) button and then **Find Document** (2).

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6270176-4nzXtjHsWIyZa4sT1zOsHT9OKDZYUpKpC7CZrbYIM4Hpkpeb1xgfLJk9XGLL0XL5-0/image-20250715162237-1.png)
2. Next, in the **Criteria** (1) field click the arrow, which will unfold the list of the possible criteria for you to use. Choose **Type** in the list box to the right of **Type**, click the arrow and select **Is one of** (2). In the last field on the line, select all the document templates (3) that you want to include in this search.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6270201-D44NgrDHCRf7BZ6wjSKCN8lR1c8KjTszhcSh2JjxoBAIAk6ulKkSMAnzxYXDxiKT-0/image-20250715165421-2.png)
3. Then you can select the period that should be considered in the selection. For example, **all documents registered for the last 5 years including this year** (1). Click **Find** (2) to list all the documents.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6270202-4ThbglWkyoP74ggizobnYC5mJ8UzrQY3z0SjyLh6j2hznh5TjDLmFfgp9w2ekzCO-0/image-20250715165617-3.png)
4. Once you have a list of documents click on the Save as selection button and [create a selection](https://docs.superoffice.com/en/search-options/selection/learn/create.html).

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6270204-aORonfjItkEgB08Ma3s37oApIrgdUMwzexZYyFGThZ6efdixo7Sp6Tf8b7zcy2jJ-0/image-20250715165840-4.png)
5. You have created a selection of documents; however, it also has a [Company/contact](https://docs.superoffice.com/en/search-options/selection/learn/index.html#tabs) tab, also known as “Shadow selection” of the companies and contacts associated with these documents. [Configure the columns](https://docs.superoffice.com/en/learn/section-tabs/configure-columns.html) in a way that you could see at least a company´s name or a contact´s name and e-mail address. This will allow you to choose the correct contacts to select later.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6270257-j7o4NewzfbfacgMWqgFbXqyL3pplPUoMT1EXr3oM61qfd5Ir7meJXsjHO0cfpDEu-0/image-20250715174142-13.png)

**Create the interest "Have documents and emails" for contacts in SuperOffice CRM**

Please read [this article](https://docs.superoffice.com/en/admin/lists/learn/index.html) to learn how to create an interest for contacts. In this example, we will use a contact interest called **“Have documents and e-mails”.**

![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6270220-We8BBGsgQq8wdqM0pM3z4dq3rHJACp3NcCC0m0GcZ81uSufYBDwSnP0sAehIuUSM-0/image-20250715170745-6.png)

**Use a bulk update to update all the contacts with documents and e-mails**

To do so, please follow the steps below:

1. To select all the contacts, first, click **Show All**, then select the first contact in the list and then scroll down until you reach the last and, while keeping the Shift key pressed, click on it. Then, right-click on selected contacts and click on **Bulk update**.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6270258-silliopTLcL0MB1loEMndHCX1J98OWwD9XRSKyQ33f4xFSmxIZ8wCWJfydIGRI3H-0/image-20250715174232-14.png)
2. Make sure you are in the **Contact (1)** tab, then select **Interest – add (2),**choose the interest you created in the previous step and click **Update (3)**. It will select this interest on all the contacts you have selected previously.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6270224-bmsbeBtMSK9mRuhMmwzbBR73hs9BBhnccSaQ2iP2sltz0kxBu7FNew731iZ2MLXw-0/image-20250715172515-10.png)

**Create a selection of all contacts in SuperOffice CRM**

Please follow [this FAQ](https://community.superoffice.com//en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-get-a-list-of-all-my-companies-or-contacts-in-superoffice-crm/) to learn step by step how to create this selection.

**Create a selection of all contacts with the "Have documents and emails" interest**

To do so, please follow the steps below:

1. Click on the **Find** (1) button and then **Find Contacts** (2).
2. Next, choose **Contact interest** in the list box to the right of **Type**, click the arrow and select **Is equal to (1)** the interest you created earlier. Click **Find (2)** and then follow the steps to [create a selection](http://docs.superoffice.com/en/search-options/selection/learn/create.html).

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6270230-DqmD5keSAsTcstws4GsxfRhxS7JKZn8t53QphKkWglz64joGTB1M5m1JrjXXYQbh-0/image-20250715173129-11.png)

**Create a combined selection with contacts without documents and/or emails**

To do so, please follow the steps below:

1. In one of the selections, you created earlier, click on the **Task** (1) button and then **Combine selection with...** (2).

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6270259-SRpbhYXY1Zkbas6s6C8XtpNqMradx7t70ymDka08oSdHfl0jcZsEanc0P5Q8ruyZ-0/image-20250715174707-15.png)
2. The new combine selection interface will be populated. Give it a meaningful name (1). As selection 1, choose **All contacts** (2); as selection 2, choose **All companies with documents and e-mails** (3). As a combination type, select **Only in Selection 1** (4) option. Click on **Save** (5) to finish.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6270260-mGZqrjo8J6JBhshbkQg3Tst9lpiwUMGswc7RjIxbHOXCqch88HrS7OT3zK6dLO6i-0/image-20250715175001-16.png)
