# How do I change our own company's name in SuperOffice CRM?

Updated on 27 Mar 2026

How do I change our own company's name (on the main company card and in the licence) in SuperOffice CRM?

To change your company's name in SuperOffice CRM, please follow the steps below:

1. Request a name change service from your Account Manager at SuperOffice or by [registering a request](https://community.superoffice.com/nl/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-register-a-support-request-from-within-superoffice-crm/) with support.
2. Receive confirmation that your company's name has been changed in our licence system.
3. The customers who work with version 8 or newer - licence information, which holds a new company´s name, will be updated automatically after logging into the [Settings and Maintenance](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-access-administration-part-settings--maintenance-in-superoffice-crm/).

   The customers who work with version 7 or older - the licence must be updated manually. Please do it within 6 days after receiving the name change confirmation.

**Note!**

- After a customer receives a notification from SuperOffice that their company´s name has been changed, they have to log in to Settings & Maintenance within 14 days.
- If you did not follow the above-described procedure, you'll get an error "Licence not found" when your licence needs to be updated.
