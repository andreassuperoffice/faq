# What to do when newly created "Sale Stage" is not visible in SuperOffice CRM?

Updated on 28 Dec 2025

I have created a new list item for **Sale - Stage**but it is not visible in SuperOffice CRM when I create a new project. Why does it happen and how can I get it visible?

To create a new Sales Stage, you need to create a new item in **Sale Stages** in Settings and Maintenance, and also link it to the **Sale Type**. To learn how to do it, follow the steps below:

1. Log in to [Settings and Maintenance](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-access-administration-part-settings--maintenance-in-superoffice-crm/).
2. Click on **Lists (1)**, select **Sale - Stage (2)** from the selection list, and click on **+Add (3).**

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6465337-ynuZcid6ndPTl49C4nLO53jHQq6JyAmsWRNWX0hO70ot90GcUHwu5L22ZoKtnuKz-0/image-20251228110928-1.png)
3. Fill in the **Sale Stage (1)** name and **Probability (2)** of the sale to be closed if it reaches this stage, **Description (3),** and click **Save (4)**to finish.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6465338-0SxoXMb4dALKcghDHOPw3cuujKcnm1Oh2WKRIk4B3JoUs0HT71rIzId358f6RW9x-0/image-20251228111142-2.png)
4. Then select**Sale-Type, Stages, Quote (1)** from the selection list, and double click on the **Sale Type (2)**to which you want to assign the sale stage that you created.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6465339-4bIjwEkBBZ9Y33fRCOTEs4wWLmt7UwmRQEwOXcmF8zmPZ7ORWjSu8aRpQUzGdC3M-0/image-20251228111302-3.png)
5. In the tab **Stages (1),**select the new **Stage (2)**you created and click **Save (3).** This will make a new stage visible under this sales type in SuperOffice CRM.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6465340-gkhYkwz0QMagrH0nsyfoPAAwPfn08U5B6DBjEAupdxaEEhEBpgPpaKgdyeRO3O3n-0/image-20251228111515-4.png)
