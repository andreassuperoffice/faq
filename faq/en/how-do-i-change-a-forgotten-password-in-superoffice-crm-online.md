# How do I change a forgotten password in SuperOffice CRM Online?

Updated on 11 May 2026

How do I change the password I forgot in SuperOffice CRM Online?

The instructions on how to change a forgotten password depend on the identity (ID) provider that controls the password:

- If you use SuperOffice as the Identity provider, please  [click here](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-change-a-forgotten-password-in-superoffice-crm-online/#1)
- If you use Microsoft as the Identity provider, please  [click here](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-change-a-forgotten-password-in-superoffice-crm-online/#2)
- If you use Google as the Identity provider, please  [click here](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-change-a-forgotten-password-in-superoffice-crm-online/#3)

**Note!** If you don´t know which identity provider you use, please contact your IT department.

​

**SuperOffice as the Identity provider**

When SuperOffice is in charge of the password, there are two ways you can change it:

- Change the password yourself:

  1. Click on **Forgot your password?** that appears after you enter your e-mail address.

  ![](https://app.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6676621-sUQgOAzbhoMQfdgE3n10TApsl7pOj5nM50ck9IWnDbS3Ftb9tGbsm1AKgCXiteLY-0/image-20260511150008-1.png)

  2. You will be redirected to <https://id.superoffice.com/forgot-password>, where you need to fill in your e-mail address and click on the **Send e-mail** button to get a password reset e-mail. Please follow the instructions in the e-mail to reset your password.

  ![](https://app.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6676962-C4jNutwZpJUsaWM4qa1oPLE0Z1p5CXP7rssI0x2zje9HUTfdXDReAjtIu1qkw1z9-0/image-20260511162528-1.png)
- Ask your SuperOffice system administrator to initiate the password reset process for you in SuperOffice [Settings and Maintenance](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-access-administration-part-settings--maintenance-in-superoffice-crm/):

  1. The administrator has to choose **Users** in the Navigator menu on the left and find your user account in the **Associate** tab.

  ![](https://app.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6676634-AhX6jC0TipmIBr5k8QDT8Jf1SJNMT7Gj1a3rHfSGrF5kEjwg3d9M6FkJxGudCTgl-0/image-20260511150126-2.png)

  2. Select your user account and click on the **Task** button at the bottom of the screen, then select the **Send Reset Password email** option. Please follow the instructions in the e-mail to reset your password.

  ![](https://app.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6676665-rmyX1UsQuSwclQOYT3G61yQqO8Yr8sih9fh72f1kAbwPfTDdOsh41YQkyNSSHXct-0/image-20260511150207-3.png)

​

**Microsoft as the Identity provider**

When Microsoft is in charge of your password, it has to be reset via the Microsoft password reset service. [This article](https://docs.microsoft.com/en-us/office365/admin/add-users/reset-passwords?view=o365-worldwide#reset-my-office-365-tenant-admin-password) explains how to reset your password in MS Office 365.

​

**Google as the Identity provider**

When Google is in charge of your password, it has to be reset via the Google password reset service. [This article](https://support.google.com/a/answer/33319?hl=en) explains how to reset your password in G Suite.
