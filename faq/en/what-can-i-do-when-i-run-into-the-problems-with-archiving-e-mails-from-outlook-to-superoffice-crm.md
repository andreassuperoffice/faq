# What can I do when I run into the problems with archiving e-mails from Outlook to SuperOffice CRM?

Updated on 3 Feb 2026

What can I do when I run into the problems with archiving e-mails from Outlook to SuperOffice CRM?

To be able to archive e-mails to SuperOffice CRM from your Outlook, make sure SuperOffice WebTools are installed, and check [this FAQ](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-download-and-install-web-tools-for-superoffice-crm/) to learn how to do it. All other most common SuperOffice Outlook Ribbon issues can be divided into two groups:

- [SuperOffice Outlook Ribbon is invisible](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/what-can-i-do-when-i-run-into-the-problems-with-archiving-e-mails-from-outlook-to-superoffice-crm/#1)
