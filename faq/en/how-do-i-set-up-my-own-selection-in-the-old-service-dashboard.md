# How do I set up my own selection in the old Service Dashboard?

Updated on 19 May 2025

I want to see all my team’s postponed requests on my dashboard. How do I do it?

To be able to pull a data report from SuperOffice Service in your Dashboard, we recommend to follow the steps below:

[Step 1. Create a new selection](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-set-up-my-own-selection-in-the-old-service-dashboard/#1)

[Step 2. Make your new selection visible in the dashboard](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-set-up-my-own-selection-in-the-old-service-dashboard/#2)

​

**Step 1. Create a new selection**

Please follow the steps below to create a new selection in Service:

1. Click on **Requests (1)** and **Find requests (2)**.

   ![](https://online2.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/4400473-ur3y9lwizNFYb9JZbE8Xljcr4aD4Wg16YaopifQS0gpCQcAUDs4sjs5dHqwiP47q-0/image.png)
2. In this example, we show how to show postponed requests, which belong to one team member. You can use **Advanced view (1)** that gives more flexibility to set up selection, then set up relevant criteria (2), (3), and click on **Save as selection** (4).

   ![](https://online2.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/4400474-aW1IndJWWSXkqTuKOD1gTThAAQAB3DtWjTMsp5EuIX7UWiRkgE6we0K7OjLIFQz3-0/image.png)
3. Fill in the **General info** tab with your preferred values, as they will help you to find this selection later on.

   ![](https://online2.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/4400475-wSmqQPPNPx3rXp8NpBQT87kqR8SQ1ujj6TJrIHGm0KSa9NAHRl93UMHwZC9HNeqy-0/image.png)
4. In the **Profile (1)** tab you can remove, add, change the size of the columns and apply various functions. When you´re done, click **OK** (2) to save your settings. [Read this article to learn more about how to manage columns.](https://help.superoffice.com/Documentation/Help/EN/CRM/10.0/UserHelp/index.htm#t=Service%2Ftopics%2Fselections.editSelection2.editProfile.html&rhsearch=columns&rhsyns=%20&rhhlterm=columns)

   ![](https://online2.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/4400476-8kgdmBAHuOtjTgswaxpkRd3U6MS0GNuIwtwQMyyUca0eZtDQ1Jo5tI03dFaNqiNF-0/image.png)

​

**Step 2. Make your selection visible in the dashboard**

Please follow the steps below to set up the selection to the Service dashboard:

1. Click on ![](https://online2.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/4400479-FnFeW4UXPDg8eMCZLMeZJUcdxOciyhflsrodRZWxkaKNuKauYMDGbDxRZPaiRhxk-0/image.png), then choose **System design** and **Web Panels** from the drop-down menu.

   ![](https://online2.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/4400480-CHTgrcFtDhwbMod0flCIRbERYTosOlAbd9ZI6cG7SSRF2KtPfgqey4H2DrglrZb5-0/image.png)
2. Click on **New web panel** next to the folder called **Main Page.**

   ![](https://online2.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/4400481-TnQMAIgHMJjUpp3jpz6Cc7s5y33dtSxbP6OwEK8bOgiNXnMZ4OSkUtYhXUgaXWjg-0/image.png)
3. Fill in the **Label field** (1), select the option **Use selection** and choose the selection you created in the previous step (2). Click **OK** (3) to save it.

   ![](https://online2.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/4400482-0oPJayzp4a1B2qRjmI2wKzo7sbt6Lo8cBMl4OGh4LymaKpiiPlGwPqSzoAiR0Xwo-0/image.png)
4. Click on the arrow in tile (1) and in a list of selections you will find your newly created selection, click on it (2) and results will appear in the tile.

   ![](https://online2.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/4400483-HJIXLjZKdxTPxOq04YwEzSuqATcOMDibHznwMosARP8lyLoSglN8SuusnG2xfycO-0/image.png)
