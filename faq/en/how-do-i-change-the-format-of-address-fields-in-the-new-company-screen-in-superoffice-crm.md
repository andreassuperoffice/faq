# How do I change the format of address fields in the new Company screen in SuperOffice CRM?

Updated on 15 May 2026

When I am creating a new company, I can fill in the visiting and postal address for the company. Most of the companies that I create have only one address. How do I change the format of the address in the Company screen?

![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6353113-WHE8ilZweTwYeUYba6XaRWJNWROQe2FLo4KLWDHxqykdyhncgE4MY8wpbFdqp94b-0/image-20251006105435-2.png)

The format in which address fields appear in SuperOffice CRM depends on the country that has been selected while creating a new company. By default, the address fields will appear in accordance with your company's registered country.

To change the address format, please follow the steps below:

1. Open the [Settings and Maintenance](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-access-administration-part-settings--maintenance-in-superoffice-crm/).
2. Click on **Lists** (1), select **General - Country** (2) from the selection list, and double-click on the country whose address format you want to change.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6353244-I44MdXK0XZjWN6teFfiiCSwKjsc3ifkuXJWh3WZJ8QAPFa74505BKLzmdSRlwBwq-0/image-20251006113558-3.png)
3. In the **Visual address format** field, you can choose a specific format for the address fields that will be displayed in a new Company screen when the country is selected.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6353256-DH2wQe4hR8OeMxz2Gm0SfT7ERPuBdglfChkYSW0Efj8dI7L2mTX0fqRO6ZFQAhEx-0/image-20251006113918-4.png)

You can find address formats for different countries in [this article](http://docs.superoffice.com/en/company/learn/address-formats.html).
