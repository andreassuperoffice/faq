# How do I setup Gmail Link and connect my Gmail account with SuperOffice CRM?

Updated on 9 May 2025

How do I setup Gmail Link and connect my Gmail account with SuperOffice CRM?

To connect your Gmail account with your SuperOffice CRM please follow the steps below:

1. Go to SuperOffice [App Store](https://online.superoffice.com/AppStore/superoffice-as/superoffice-gmail-link).
2. Click on the **Download for Chrome/Firefox (depending on your browser)**button on the left side of the menu.
   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6200272-K3YmLb8yCp4GTHgd4bBgCMAt3ZCCSQfEt2KurJW91PkngTnPRb3sNQRm07Ll6bmq-0/image-20250509153617-1.png)

   The example below will show how to install it on the **Chrome** browser.
3. Click on the **Add to Chrome** button.
   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6200273-GlSYsAAgwhllGnhLAqgdFuNjplISDIN08B4t8megoWP8KQaNOeLKHnixT7phx200-0/image-20250509153850-2.png)
4. A pop up of your browser will appear asking if you want to add your Gmail Link extension. Click **Add extension**.
   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6200274-Q5N7Ywggc3n5GHF5oJzoahiyqkIjnJqMRSeCzHdCI4fuW5nU0ejakuynzkkae2Nx-0/image-20250509153940-3.png)
5. Then you will be asked to configure Gmail Link with your installation. Click **Login (1)**when you use SuperOffice Online. **Click here to configure to an onsite installation (2)** if you use SuperOffice Onsite.
   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6200275-XrGtsLsdptMdsBaFplTgxBnSX7dQSNPjsiqXc5zUHxmhN3K8fGfgeFnMaWdSJTp5-0/image-20250509154039-4.png)
6. Then log in to your Gmail account. SuperOffice Gmail Link will appear on the right top corner of the e-mail as in the screenshot below.
   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6200295-8KcyLhaceClZWHaeDLlRYY9qfchRwRxskS8WEm5ueaoeJ1mmfR9x8YIpAJxdPd5U-0/image-20250509154947-5.png)
7. Before starting archiving incoming documents and emails in SuperOffice from your Gmail we suggest making sure that your Gmail Link settings are set up in the way that you want.
   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6200324-fR8gd5S173wpA4IazdtNuQ3jOLf224Hfac8eLfPY0fUpQ3DczPujjEZ1WF0xqlHi-0/image-20250509160415-6.png)
