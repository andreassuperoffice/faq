# How do I create contacts without a company connection?

Updated on 28 Dec 2025

I am creating a new contact, but I cannot save it. Why does it happen, and how do I create it?

![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6465331-dnzMLMtfxMqaGw05exYYmABYKLF7DaiPHhbrmkaKrJ5ftqEAYAeujXexk3HqNfjK-0/image-20251228103115-2.png)

To be able to save a contact without assigning it to a company record, you need to enable a preference that allows this. Please follow the steps below to do it:

1. Log in to [Settings & Maintenance](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-access-administration-part-settings--maintenance-in-superoffice-crm/).
2. Click on **Preferences (1),** then **Global preferences (2),**and next to **Mandatory company on contact (3),** choose **No.**
