# How do I make some of my activities invisible from other SuperOffice users (private)?

Updated on 21 Jun 2025

I have some confidential information in some appointment description, which I wouldn't like to be accessible to my colleagues. Is it possible to hide it in SuperOffice CRM?

You can easily create follow-ups, documents, and sales and make records visible only to yourself or the users in your primary group. To be able to do it, please follow the steps below:

1. Login to [Settings and Maintenance](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-access-administration-part-settings--maintenance-in-superoffice-crm/) and open **Users**(1) and select the user (2) which should have this feature available.
2. Then click on the **Licenses**(3)tab add a tick next to **Confidential activities** (4) and **Save** (5)changes.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6246676-lBTyhMqiSUtotyKnpGyTxEGk3n3frCRO4CqZMPSbbt86nWDHbR9Fy4IDSwTQ84sA-0/image-20250621133025-1.png)
3. Now, users who have a license for confidential activities can create follow-ups and documents by selecting a value in the **Visible for** field.
   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6246677-GPzRDPNiq54mmpOJQU3ZEZnsGuZdlfMpYCpODC20JB9rXbMFK5Rb7SdlmKDtCzz5-0/image-20250621133908-2.png)
4. In the activities section tab the follow-ups, which are visible only for you will appear in *italics* font.

![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6246678-SGtaSd6mYY5lJSHGSp2AhydnmIhIOOADg2MqhMtS7zGxAzHKDlemSQWG13BOyA4E-0/image-20250621134331-3.png)

**Note!** Users may need to logout and login again before they will see the **Visible for** field. It can also be that the field is hidden for your user group or for a certain follow-up type. In such cases, an administrator has to add this field via [Screen Designer](https://docs.superoffice.com/en/ui/screen-designer/learn/index.html).
