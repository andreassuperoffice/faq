# How do I register a support request from within SuperOffice CRM?

Updated on 13 May 2025

What is the easiest way to register a support request to SuperOffice Support?

If you use any type of SuperOffice CRM which is newer or SuperOffice 8 you can easily submit a new support request within your SuperOffice CRM by following the steps below. Follow the steps below to learn how to do it:

1. Click on the **Help (1)** buttonand in the drop-down menu click **Contact** s**upport (2).**

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6202150-BjhJiOsmoJHlSgLFBhYldZ3YYq8tjJ02sGQXdadACyhyUpBHe7TBWRjfLxzdaaqX-0/image-20250513093607-1.png)
2. In the **Register request** screen, fill in all the required fields: **Choose the topic you need help with** **(1)** and write your **question** or describe the **situation you are experiencing (2)**. On the right side of the screen, you will see the **FAQ suggestions (3)** that are selected based on your problem description.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6202269-x0BljqDQPgNN4eNXeOnontUqR4YWlPMEfIw4nClMMfGEsYpE4y5speXjKDxJ3KjU-0/image-20250513095948-6.png)
3. You can click on the FAQ title and read it in the same window without losing the description you already wrote.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6202222-XUlCDBcIt7nkq2WT2k6GHo3YxZ2PxBHj6DFULYSSxP0c1FbCp7fS2z5fG6PAxruq-0/image-20250513094603-4.png)|

   If the FAQ did not help, then fill in the field **Tell us a bit more about your issue (4)** so that support can help you faster.
4. Click the **Send (1)** button. Once the request has been registered, you will get a pop-up confirmation screen. You can then **Close (2)** the screen.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6202689-hULkSk52Ob5tCCCd9k5Dp99UXLZqloesUsBzEIfJsaZdpwnfIH3pyjkT0BDR81TJ-0/image-20250513113820-7.png)
