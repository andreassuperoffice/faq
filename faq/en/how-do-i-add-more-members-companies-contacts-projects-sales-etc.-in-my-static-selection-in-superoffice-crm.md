# How do I add more members (companies, contacts, projects, sales, etc.) in my static selection in SuperOffice CRM?

Updated on 28 Dec 2025

I have created a static selection of my contacts that I haven’t called in a year. While I was working through the list, I realized that a few more people need to be followed up with, but they do not fit this criterion. How do I include these contacts in my static selection?

There are different ways in which you can include more contacts in your static selection. Please follow the steps in the sections below:

- [Add contacts using search criteria](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-add-more-members-companies-contacts-projects-sales-etc.-in-my-static-selection-in-superoffice-crm/#1)
- [Add contacts manually](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-add-more-members-companies-contacts-projects-sales-etc.-in-my-static-selection-in-superoffice-crm/#2)

**Add contacts using search criteria**

When you want to add new selection members using search criteria, you want to use the **Find screen** to define criteria for adding members that fulfill the criteria and then add these members to your selection. Please follow the steps below to add more contacts to your static selection:

1. Make a search in the Find Screen and open the **Results** (1) tab. Then click on the **Task** (2) button and select the **Copy into selection** (3) option.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6465360-WZRclwDHFobErshO7CRwJQfRDKQ4aSZOBJN3zkzhlF4carSBMRcYZXhUgxsUMb6L-0/image-20251228123238-2.png)
2. Select **Copy into existing static selection** (1) option and choose your **Selection** (2) from the drop-down list, click **OK** (3) to copy the contacts.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6465361-if9l1iX89iFPcCL6RJtcgCqjNStPyJypgi4mtymfNkgGw8rX25NmbZegWdlbZe2r-0/image-20251228123701-3.png)

**Add contacts manually**

If you only want to add specific members to a static selection, you can do that from various places in SuperOffice CRM. Please follow the steps in the sections below to add more contacts to your static selection:

- **From the Company screen**

  Go to the Company screen, select the contact in the **Contact** (1) section tab, right-click on the contact (2), and choose **Add to selection** (3).

  ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6465362-0P1u8ZaaagbqDTBA1aJUWecWYNaYkw3ZWq7DODNDUNzx8JaZyxesM4R1WOtdmNFh-0/image-20251228123855-4.png)
- **From the Contact screen**

  Go to the Contact screen, click on the Task button, and select **Add to selection.**

  ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6465363-uJISSx1u7YRfUuXhb724grMideoHxCHYtPsBuE8fuYLU2oJmd7jbQJCkJKMNuXW0-0/image-20251228124012-5.png)
- **From all section tabs containing contacts**

  Open a section tab containing contacts (e.g., the **Project Members** (1) section tab in the Project screen. **Select one or more contacts** (2), right-click, and choose **Add to selection** (3).

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6465364-kTIDXFarkHshSPBy8R6l0r9mZZX0NTBLOz0UmPPlzSK80EBAkdiwxr4FlcEgzPrG-0/image-20251228124228-6.png)
- **From any selection screen**

  Open a selection consisting of the contacts you want to include in your static selection, select the contact (press the CTRL button on your keyboard and keep selecting the contacts from the list). When you select all the contacts, right-click and click on the option **Add to selection**

  ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6465366-Tf6269rPWGDDTT15xT4ozkEyyrqrdPY6bqGkFU6o2j5i9ZMbWZwAG8FR73rtEdbT-0/image-20251228124629-8.png)
- **From the dynamic selection screen**

  Open a selection consisting of the contacts you want to include in your static selection, then click on the **Task** (1) button, and then select **Copy into selection** (2).

  ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6465367-leda3U7Ya3NB4fIoexZ9Mh84JPDFKS2uRjnQSgwyuLYZgA6ZS2W7nOYkSWzdgWOf-0/image-20251228124946-9.png)
- **From the static selection screen**

  Open a selection, then click on the **Add Members** (1) button.

  ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6465368-Ccrj5cz1OKtZ3MrO1DNx4LEkxCNQPf9bPfkw3Cw4rdmOQfs1zp88kyeKuxMe1Lt1-0/image-20251228125111-10.png)

  Then select the criteria (1) that would find all the members you want to include in the selection and click **OK** (2). The member will be included automatically.

  ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6465369-JLiOixDkg9MBiItjA6MceTkYJDlZWJytWLq4cps0NJ402YnceBji2Kgfcfa5aMZ4-0/image-20251228125224-11.png)

  You will see a message stating the number of new members added to the selection. Click **OK** to close it.

  ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6465370-o7RXcMhh71k2uqD6EextJtwK4I7RXOtOPdb26NBeG1W5Lyd8RtjLcRyb6a0l47U4-0/image-20251228125249-12.png)
