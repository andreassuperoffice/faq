# How do I access administration part (Settings & Maintenance) in SuperOffice CRM?

Updated on 28 Apr 2026

How do I access administration part (Settings & Maintenance) in SuperOffice CRM?

To be able to access Settings and maintenance your SuperOffice user has to belong to the [user group](https://docs.superoffice.com/en/admin/user-management/learn/role/index.html) that has administration [functional rights](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/whichare-all-availablefunctional-rights-in-superoffice-and-what-each-of-them-mean/). Please check [this FAQ](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-configure-what-information-the-users-of-our-company-can-access-in-superoffice-crm/) to learn more about how to configure access rights in your SuperOffice.

When your user has the necessary rights, you can access the administration part by clicking on the button  ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6200350-FQ9IN89cT0NP4iO8S038axamSoXHYIi5aKRbilyFoNxlBdWaTZiDzQ343amUhSxo-0/image-20250509161629-1.png) at the top right corner of the SuperOffice screen and selecting **Settings and maintenance** from the drop-down menu.

![](https://app.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6652614-RE90JiuiOJCgRjOemQzLLhOiXhLR5pXsIj9udqyqkbr0POzwiDet6FA7SND7uJ6r-0/image-20260428122549-1.png)

**Note!**

1. From SuperOffice 8, the full administration module is web-based (accessible only via the browser).
2. To learn how to use the administration module in SuperOffice CRM, you can check our [articles](https://docs.superoffice.com/en/learn/administration.html).
3. The URL examples to access the administration module are:

- For Onsite: [Your installation URL]admin/default.aspx?
- For Online: https://online.superoffice.com/[YourCustID]/admin/default.aspx?

If you don't know the URL to your SuperOffice Web installation, contact your company's IT department.
