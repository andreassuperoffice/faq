# How do I reset a login in Pocket CRM app?

Updated on 25 Feb 2026

I want to connect a different SuperOffice CRM solution to my Pocket CRM app. How do I do it?

To reset a Pocket CRM login, please check[this video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DmQW8Q8VEaU&list=PL9fefjwA0X29b2fAf6l_BvsZLFGllomCk&index=5) or follow the steps below:

1. Find the SuperOffice (Pocket CRM) app among your apps, open it, and tap on the **Storage** option.

   ![](https://online2.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/4534826-LKlwgoCzKDQaOpbaxoWaDwZrkww32wiU35ww2OGh6ZZkRoxh32gj0rgBMIw4zXpd-0/image-20201202183717-5.png)
2. Tap on the **Clear Data** option and confirm the action if necessary.

   ![](https://online2.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/4534827-584UDmXdHWRB3Ew83Uxsd6NtMEfeFbYU2g4EPXBHrlSUJ7Ize9XlYU04aQZMT7cn-0/image-20201202183726-6.png)

   ![](https://online2.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/4534828-tfHn9bDhjHrjD8ywZXGAdor5zrou1wbAuZzNfWgCoMdn9q0sqf8CZE6TSEa3PW4u-0/image-20201202183733-7.png)
3. Start your SuperOffice app again and choose the installation type and configure the connection (2).

   ![](https://online2.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/4534829-R8K9J3DYBb0JXJtFMUPE5OhKX6NInz35ScQrseUKlocNukQbeoaLGqbKKyf1qKwE-0/image-20201202183745-8.png)

   ![](https://online2.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/4534830-g5rXnZFFDWRHWRb9joFSOppZhsbX6dOBulTfhn52UFGrw95fiK1FR822CfwgqXs8-0/image-20201202183754-9.png)

**Note!** If you use Pocket CRM on the iPhone, to clear the app cache from the device, you need to uninstall the app entirely and then reinstall it.

[Mobile CRM](https://appstore.superoffice.com/superoffice-as/superoffice-mobile-crm) is available for all customers using version 9 or higher of SuperOffice CRM. In this app, you only need to log out; resetting your login is not required.
