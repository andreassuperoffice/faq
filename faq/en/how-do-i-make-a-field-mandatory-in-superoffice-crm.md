# How do I make a field mandatory in SuperOffice CRM?

Updated on 28 Dec 2025

I want our users to always fill in some particular fields in SuperOffice. How do I make fields in SuperOffice CRM mandatory?

The procedure of making fields mandatory in SuperOffice depends on the type of the field:

- [Standard fields](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-make-a-field-mandatory-in-superoffice-crm/#1)
- [User-defined fields](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-make-a-field-mandatory-in-superoffice-crm/#2)

​

**Standard fields**

To make standard (out-of-the-box) SuperOffice fields mandatory, advanced configuration is necessary, which can be performed by SuperOffice consultants.

**Note!** The consultant service has a fee. Please contact your SuperOffice contact person if you would like to get more information about this service.

​

**User-defined fields**

You can make a user-defined field mandatory to fill in by adding a tick next to an option **Mandatory (1)** when creating the field. Don't forget to click **Save (2)**to apply changes.
