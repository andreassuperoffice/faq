# How do I move a contact to another company or leave it with a blank company field?

Updated on 9 Oct 2025

How do I move a contact to another company or leave it with a blank company field?

To change the Company on a Contact card, please follow the steps below:

1. Click on the **Task** button in the Contact card, and select **Manage contacts** and then **Move contact**.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6359501-JHpPP6KuFO6CBmdaW0IQKKTh5BObNgyelP1wpIqNaBaoGaLOouqyarDCf1HEc6Ku-0/image-20251009194629-1.png)
2. Under **Action on contact**, select one of the following options:

- Select **Move to** and choose the company you want to move the contact to.
- Select **Disconnect from <company>**.

      3. Select one of the following under Action on all existing activities and sales:

- **Leave all on <company>.** Select this option if you do not want to transfer the activities and sales along with the contact, but keep them on the current company.
- **Move all to....** Select this option if you want to transfer all the activities and sales to a new company. If you are assigning the contact to a new company, all the activities and sales are transferred together with the contact.
- **Move all activities starting from.** Select this option if you want to transfer activities and sales starting from a specific date.

      4. Click on **Move (3)** and the contact is disconnected from the company.

![](https://online2.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/4400991-Ze6IlF1ulnoEQFgmiJm3r7N7Ynp8DmXU7wdhbmaKmf87jGouuNOybrM9r25zaDpH-0/image.png)

**Note!**

1. To access this function, the user must have the functional right **Can manage (move and merge) entities**.

2. To disconnect a contact from the company, you must have a set preference that allows creating a contact without the company. Check [this FAQ](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-create-contacts-without-a-company-connection/) for more information.

3. It is possible to use a [Bulk Update function](https://docs.superoffice.com/en/learn/basics/bulk-update.html) to disconnect contacts from companies in bulk.
