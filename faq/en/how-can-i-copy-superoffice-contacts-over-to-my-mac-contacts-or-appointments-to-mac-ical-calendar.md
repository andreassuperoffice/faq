# How can I copy SuperOffice contacts over to my Mac Contacts or appointments to Mac iCal Calendar?

Updated on 25 Mar 2026

How can I copy SuperOffice contacts to my Mac Contacts or appointments to Mac iCal calendar?

To start the process, make sure that SuperOffice WebTools are installed. Check [this FAQ](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-download-and-install-webtools-for-superoffice-crm/) for more information. Then

[To learn how to copy SuperOffice contacts to Mac Contacts, click on this section](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-can-i-copy-superoffice-contacts-over-to-my-mac-contacts-or-appointments-to-mac-ical-calendar/#1)

[To learn how to copy SuperOffice appointments to the Mac iCal Calendar, click on this section](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-can-i-copy-superoffice-contacts-over-to-my-mac-contacts-or-appointments-to-mac-ical-calendar/#2)

​

**Learn how to copy SuperOffice contacts to Mac Contacts**

1. [Create a selection](https://community.superoffice.com/en/customer/learn/standard/selections/dynamic-selections/) in SuperOffice of the contacts you wish to copy to Mac Contacts.
2. Open SuperOffice WebTools App (the owl icon on the system toolbar) and click on the **Prefere****nces** option.

    ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6604628-oGzScMgslNR7eS1IwRLsxb32OPf8S0Msr2m7iB5mUk9X9BnIbznqzikdpgAwo6iP-0/image-20260325163442-1.png)
3. In the Superoffice WebTools Preference window, the **Address book** section, **From selection field** (1), select the selection that has been created in SuperOffice CRM and click **Copy now** (2).

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6604633-CUqwWmZ6Xk3Nr7OGGXsoXK055dtziqTj9EMs3lduw7ttads8uqY5Ime2LpdJPilg-0/image-20260325163836-5.png)

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6604630-4mPregQOTeGSirHBgRjIWDppHfSr7JxfU2Rj8WrshlXWp2wdFTD7q3plrk3f2KKa-0/image-20260325163616-3.png)

​

**Learn how to copy SuperOffice appointments to the Mac iCal Calendar**

1. Open SuperOffice WebTools App (the owl icon on the system toolbar) and click on the **Prefere****nces** option.

    ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6604628-oGzScMgslNR7eS1IwRLsxb32OPf8S0Msr2m7iB5mUk9X9BnIbznqzikdpgAwo6iP-0/image-20260325163442-1.png)
2. In the Superoffice WebTools Preference window, the **Calendar**section chooses the options, which will be applied to copied appointments and click **Copy now**.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6604634-oQkATSKTgIzOfLmJ6AlNzcD7B3kHkSxh4ITFTwP9QWKfk1xdAf8OqmHA5nlWTI2s-0/image-20260325163928-6.png)

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6604647-c208gyNWN0u0xgpxcW7GMTlDgCynH4OWFyreQbGYQlbPgpiRW07ntzl0OoBYu0qL-0/image-20260325164101-7.png)
3. This will copy one month back and all future appointments forward from SuperOffice to the Mac iCal calendar.

   **Note!**If no appointments were copied, make sure that in Security & Privacy settings in the Calendars section, SuperOffice Web tools has access (tick next to application) to your calendar.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6604629-PFIECL0Rj6hZYio1fFO78CbUrb53i6F5XlRTpNKGHXc6TAYNZusuEtI98cTuZ2m2-0/image-20260325163544-2.png)
