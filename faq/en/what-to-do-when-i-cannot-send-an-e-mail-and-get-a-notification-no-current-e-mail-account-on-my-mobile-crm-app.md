# What to do when I cannot send an e-mail and get a notification "No current e-mail account" on my Mobile CRM app?

Updated on 28 Dec 2025

I try to send an e-mail using my Mobile CRM, but I get a notification *"An error occurred: No current e-mail account".*Why do I get it, and how do I fix it?

![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6465371-zCd4zcZbByOfP2BGIAWeIc2QBqCBxlrwCBdU2gA1NL8dwWHYE2JcttS2aAKnlkhM-0/image-20251228131041-1.png)

The message pops up when your e-mail account is not configured in SuperOffice CRM. Please follow the steps below to configure them:

1. Click **OK** on the information message, and you will land on the **E-mail settings** screen.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6465372-nyMDcMYSSP7qzQlKpwjuqJbhMOlobdRkfJoemCSYLZnlKjM0XScdqZp2yHzQYi5U-0/image-20251228131235-2.png)
2. Tap on the **E-mail client** field, and there you can choose to use either the **System default** (1) or the **SuperOffice** (2) e-mail client.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6465373-y0FHzug9nbNTqQdLEtWgtWIBZ38FasBiuaTD2yY50alacGirw7aWf7Wg57TNc7xg-0/image-20251228131343-3.png)
   - Please follow the instructions below when you choose to use the **SuperOffice** e-mail client:

- When your default e-mail client is **SuperOffice**, please follow the steps in the relevant FAQ:
  - [How do I start using the SuperOffice Inbox?](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-start-using-the-superoffice-inbox/)
  - [How to set up my Office 365 email account with SuperOffice Inbox?](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-to-set-up-my-office-365-email-account-withsuperofficeinbox/)
- When your default e-mail client in SuperOffice CRM is **Microsoft Outlook**, please follow the steps below:
  1. Click on ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6465374-Ru7ESr4ukDLFIZoRSwIQc6cOAoSDiuY94JTUERpy2PcWLuZ0597cX3o7GN4P5r1Y-0/image-20251228131456-4.png) the button and choose **Local settings****.**

     ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6465375-PE0GxRmx7dwmZe0gFgh484WzbkByagD2wpgPZ3Ul0B67liMeJ215AIEMlsCDMakX-0/image-20251228131537-5.png)
  2. Choose the **SuperOffice CRM** option and click the **OK** button.

     ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6465376-hJQcz8nNX7Y2xoncbty6J9mSc8eda1coHhkaOh2szee0Jip6j2DASlFFi4RUtNuY-0/image-20251228131648-6.png)
  3. Configure the e-mail client following the instructions in the FAQ above.
  4. Repeat the first two steps of this part and reset your e-mail client to **Microsoft Outlook**.
