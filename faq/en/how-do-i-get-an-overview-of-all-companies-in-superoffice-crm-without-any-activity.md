# How do I get an overview of all companies in SuperOffice CRM without any activity?

Updated on 7 Apr 2025

I want to find all companies in SuperOffice CRM for which we did not create any activity. How do I get an overview like this?

In SuperOffice CRM there are these types of activities:

- Follow-ups (appointments, tasks and phone calls)
- Documents (e-mails, letters, and other documents)
- Mailings (bulk mailings by merged document, e-mail or SMS)
- Other (chat records, form submissions)

You have two ways to get an overview of companies in your CRM that do not have any activities:

1. [Get it by using Activity Monitor (SAINT) criteria](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-get-an-overview-of-all-companies-in-superoffice-crm-without-any-activity/#1)
2. [Get it by using Combined Selection](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-get-an-overview-of-all-companies-in-superoffice-crm-without-any-activity/#2)

**Note!** Activity Monitors a.k.a Sales Intelligence (SAINT) and Combined Selections are available in Premium plans of SuperOffice Online. If you use SuperOffice Onsite subscription and SAINT is not included in your license plan yet, please contact your SuperOffice representative.

​

#### **Get it by using Activity Monitor (SAINT) criteria**

To begin with, your SuperOffice CRM must have SAINT (Sales intelligence) counters enabled. To do so, please follow the steps below:

1. Go to [Settings and maintenance.](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-access-administration-part-settings--maintenance-in-superoffice-crm/)
2. Click on **SAINT**(1) and in **Counters** (2) tab enable **Counters** (3). Now set up periods of your interest, for example: 30 days, 60 days and 90 days (4). Do it for all the SuperOffice entities that you want to do reporting on and then **Regenerate all status monitors** (5). It will activate your criteria.
   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6166110-2uaMWLrk0ScQqxb9AipgUwRFi028ycznY7UTytEAdKmqTuzIWX9cNsFc9jnC3ufY-0/image-20250407172209-1.png)

Once the SAINT criteria have been created you can use them in search. To do so, please follow the steps below:

1. Click on the **Find** (1) button and then **Find Companies** (2).

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6166115-3TMchbiD9NAws9wQ7eZ41KST9YW8qo5hi7DUXZdk7WHH6sXEW9BJGrlKisL2YZG4-0/image-20250407172536-2.png)
2. Next, the **Criteria** (1)field click the arrow, which will unfold the list of the possible criteria for you to use. You will find the **Counter (SAINT)** (2)section and underneath a list of various pre-calculated criteria related to activities under companies. In this example, we will use **Number of activities in last 90 days** (3).

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6166116-Adb7I206FrK4SspeNK3ts1gX2qWuZqZyZhqAK0auhD4NTGPoGA1SHe3ezcEBXnze-0/image-20250407172848-3.png)
3. Then you can select that **Number of activities in last 90 days** should be equal to 0.  When you click on the **Find** (2)button, the search with this criteria will bring you a list of all companies that do not have any activity recorded in the last 90 days.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6166117-grg7yXJATlRz8ECTlEGrf1T1HT6R8xfQR3OZ5OltUwljez5ZH1RBfQc1zwLZcqSz-0/image-20250407173016-4.png)
4. You can save this search as a selection that can be reused later. If you don't save it, the search will be lost once you will change the criteria to make another search.
   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6166121-t1WIbsTSlHPqaKw7q5TzLkie39PkClySKtT41lAu2BN3nYEbNHPopBIKrTMbf57s-0/image-20250407173333-5.png)

#### **Get it by using Combined Selection**

Another, way to get an overview of all the companies without activities is by using a search with multiple sets of criteria. It is more time consuming, but in some ways, a more flexible way. To do so, please follow the steps below:

1. Click on the **Find** (1) button and then **Find Company** (2).

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6166122-ITZim17RN2zrg3JeEOSL224yJwdpwXCtMOiQqBH4GxKswpE0l6mlq40ao3r1jimY-0/image-20250407173351-6.png)
2. Then find our suggested criteria **Follow-up Date** (1) and set it to **Previous 90 days** (2) and click **OR** (3)

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6166125-cD7tdaDlIzXSjAM1O1DxPJ05CNmBo0g6JpZnnDNnRbSfxE9uAL8IFfRGYkysM3Rm-0/image-20250407173644-7.png)
3. Then you will be able to set other criteria. We suggest using the **Document Registered date** (1) and use the same time frame as in the previous criteria. After clicking **Find** you will get a list of all companies that HAD registered follow-ups and documents in the last 90 days.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6166132-qYiLBuM3uG9NHE9zECdUDdATDtKZnQlpugAxJsKJFDuZKc2qt9KZnCOKjCGk748I-0/image-20250407173758-8.png)
4. Save this search as a selection, by clicking on the **Save to selection** button, choosing the selection type, giving it a meaningful name (1) and clicking **Save** (2).

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6166250-gLt7fPQ3IhiDNYZlrlSQ2i3w8eYLA5Qsq3syy1Sgf71miUBJp1UjK5HA84L3F2Mz-0/image-20250407185150-9.png)
5. Now you have to create a selection containing all existing companies and use this selection in combination with the previous one. To list all existing companies in SuperOffice CRM, create a new search using the criteria **Company Name Starts with %** and save it to the same type of selection as you chose earlier. Give it a meaningful name and save it.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6166251-8nQpkPkKpPZxZwKZzWTSq9IZIrgE777SiUnJtryhHkb1imz0IM5PkCzSIpoBTTzL-0/image-20250407185435-10.png)
6. Then you want to combine these selections and list only companies that do not have follow-ups and documents. Open the selection you created in the previous step and click on **Task button**(1) and **Combine selection with..** (2).

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6166252-cZstedEJRjuMG3eQRPtS7U2Qa0gCjoXBQwdFyRZMCBU5NB9QCht3Ql8OeSKTNB7j-0/image-20250407185600-11.png)
7. In the field **Selection 1** (1) choose the selection which lists all the companies. In the field **Selection 2** (2) choose the selection which lists all the companies with documents and follow-ups. From the **Compare** drop-down choose **Only in Selection 1** (3), give a meaningful name (4) and **Save** (5) the selection. Now you got a list of all companies that do not have any documents and follow-ups in the specified time.
   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6166253-XEbsUcENZfgb9nUNAML1c94OHgqTyuNaezXQlpRgKsMbyXNRF5tfS9xx5xx99NY5-0/image-20250407190205-12.png)
