# How do I insert a subscription management link in the privacy confirmation e-mail template?

Updated on 6 Oct 2025

How do I insert an e-marketing subscription management link in the privacy confirmation e-mail template?

To insert an e-marketing subscription management link, please follow the steps below:

1. In [Settings and Maintenance](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-access-administration-part-settings--maintenance-in-superoffice-crm/), click on the **Lists (1)** button, choosean **Email template (2),** then double-click on a template called **Confirmation - Contact added**.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6354126-tzOY77mc0i2dC4khxyDDWDiqPpm0hFoRlSaRbKgMk8BpnpOF33jhzmGPT1dDq8W9-0/image-20251006161146-1.png)
2. Click **Edit (1)** to edit the English version of a template. Click on the **Languages tab (2)**, select a language (3) translation, and click the **Edit (4)** button to edit a particular language translation. You can also find the English version of the template in translations. You can edit the template from there as well.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6354128-SiOaddALZSyoGLaOrCqBLsUTLSrHjN197ZY0dU2PKOeZwfphSwe6kkBfhn9UWrLE-0/image-20251006161552-2.png)
3. Select your **text (1)**, click on the **Insert link icon (2),** and in the URL field type in the variable {asmu}, which will redirect a customer to his/her personal e-marketing subscription management landing page.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6354152-aaopN77d6BbgCrnQQjogpqjb7sazKfXbdyr9RKTdw97YbbfMHwj5ZG5Gc4ZPzLM9-0/image-20251006161922-3.png)
4. The link will be valid for 3 weeks after you send out a privacy confirmation e-mail. After the 3 weeks, your customers will still be able to open the link, but they will see the following message:

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6354196-1unxLG2csOwf1xzkKeGsl4EGoUcDE0ob7XIeyw3ejuZraWqmAatwTETc7zuFAbrr-0/image-20251006163543-4.png)

   **Note! "Click here to receive a new link to your primary e-mail address "**link will be available for 60 days only. After this period, it will not be possible to get a new link, unless you send out a new privacy confirmation e-mail.
