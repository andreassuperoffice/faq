# How do I create and use a privacy confirmation e-mail template?

Updated on 20 Oct 2025

How do I create and use a privacy confirmation e-mail template?

You can create your own privacy confirmation e-mail template with different language translations and automatically use it when new contacts are created in your SuperOffice CRM. Please follow the steps below to do it:

[Step 1. Create a new privacy e-mail template](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-create-and-use-a-privacy-confirmation-e-mail-template/#1)

[Step 2. Create translations of your privacy e-mail template](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-create-and-use-a-privacy-confirmation-e-mail-template/#2)

[Step 3. Assign this e-mail template to Privacy - Source list item](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-create-and-use-a-privacy-confirmation-e-mail-template/#3)

​

**Step 1. Create a new privacy e-mail template**

To create a new privacy confirmation e-mail template, please follow the steps below:

1. Log in to [Settings and Maintenance](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-access-administration-part-settings--maintenance-in-superoffice-crm/) in SuperOffice CRM.
2. Click on the **Lists**(1) button in the Navigator panel, then select **E-mail templates** (2) from the drop-down list and click on **+Add** (3).

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6370096-EtBJjRAgXB9rSsN2ndGSOKg7O1WyiIBb233QjswjY1kdtdnG7BOlPEoMIO6sHUZo-0/image-20251020185513-1.png)
3. Fill in the name for your template (1), choose a *Privacy confirmation* (2) type of the template, select **Create a new e-mail template** (3), if you wish to create a template from scratch, and click **Save**(4).

   **Note!**You also have options to upload .htm/.html templates from your computer or copy an existing e-mail template from SuperOffice CRM.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6370099-pXg9X6FjRm0gX9Y9Y9NEEIMqiIEBE1DcLH9lWOzu4IP4Qnct2ub3eO9J7poRHxKS-0/image-20251020185851-2.png)
4. Click on **Edit**to open the e-mail text editor.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6370100-GBr9StGXzDgKo5fc6wwKaqpDxdpc479JKZ74cAkhQt13e7u5jT8Zpr3ykISPZpu9-0/image-20251020185936-3.png)
5. Fill in your e-mail template with your text. In your e-mail templates, you can use variables, which will automatically fill in information from SuperOffice CRM click on the ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6370101-ujsA0w0OkuDfoWTF69URLj8RZ5XumcjXBBrdwG0SEPZWpYPuKlsOCKoRCG4YBTxm-0/image-20251020190319-4.png) button (1) to get a list of all available variables. Also, you can easily include a subscription management link to your privacy template (2). Check [this FAQ](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-insert-a-subscription-management-link-in-the-privacy-confirmation-e-mail-template/) to learn how. When you are done, click on the **Save**(3) button to finish.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6370102-wXNTW8HzaSQ6Aj7YTXLyzNigLlnJA4zznbqDMzTEBHYqKcFWMQqBdDb4u7ubTzCy-0/image-20251020190617-5.png)
6. Fill in the **Subject**(1) line of your template.

   Then select which **From**(2)address should be used when this e-mail is sent out.You can choose to always use a specified e-mail address, use our contact where specified, use our support contact where specified, or use the logged-in user.

   Then, in the field **Otherwise use** (3) fill in an e-mail address, which will be used always or as a backup if the above selected setting brings up empty results.

   Next, choose the **Direction**(4) of *Outgoing*.

   Then, select **Intention**(5) of the e-mail. Intention field lists all the intentions that you have defined in the *Follow-up – Intentions* list.

   Choose where the document template should be stored (6).

   Fill in **Our reference**(7) field if you want to use a sequence number from the e-mail template you are using, and click **Save**(8) to finish.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6370108-sY5ml51Wswi2Z1iheKILuX2RHbwoKBsaYDPykTIPRLWdlCXRHdesPOcSBrleFHW1-0/image-20251020191244-6.png)

​

**Step 2. Create translations of your privacy e-mail template**

To create translations of the privacy confirmation e-mail template, please follow the steps below:

1. Click on the **Language**(1)tab, then click on the **+Add** (2).

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6370109-LuGJXGMInkQgfuo9bSE5rgOq1CmtUAdqPkOO892DSd9CGL7t5ypCPSkAi7bSeBlL-0/image-20251020191438-7.png)
2. In the **Add language** (1) field, it's quick to change the language of your e-mail template by choosing to **Copy an existing e-mail template file** (2), then clicking **Add** (3)to go further.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6370110-8uf6qxfFiN2UfiW2sAddkAS9pAAA5Sr4YhifGsnjPbMa1i0E4yTkAEeU4Pj2ozO8-0/image-20251020191609-8.png)
3. Select the language from the list (1) and click **Edit** (2). The e-mail text editor will open, where you'll be able to type in the text of the e-mail template translation. When you are done with the translations of this template, click **Save** (3) to finish.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6370111-elf6nPuqd0B3EaH5SlgQ1pB19baZsrFwqLiXPHweQ0RKzCk2ZddFsOT8q01kw3j0-0/image-20251020191751-9.png)

​

**Step 3. Assign this e-mail template to Privacy - Source list item**

To set up a system such that the e-mail template you have created before would be used automatically, please follow the steps below:

1. Click on the**Lists (1)** in the drop-down menu, select **Privacy - Source (2),**and double-click on the item that should have your new privacy e-mail template assigned. In this example, we want all contacts **Created by user (3)** would get the privacy e-mail, which we have created.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6370112-xZ68JADSFE3GsCeSzHwyzYl72n4Q2Z7AfK0K37Nt85IKIFSDmrq16e8PSd2N6jMx-0/image-20251020191953-10.png)
2. In the item dialog, select the checkbox next to **Automatically send confirmation e-mail to contact (1)**. It will enable the template field. Select your new e-mail template from the list (2) and click **Save (3)**.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6370113-POwDOBtPoqaQlfCz4PUhIntaai7CKrxJIFfDlj0r6CkENz9oz9XsIqNyU2tcSQ53-0/image-20251020192305-11.png)

You can also send out the privacy confirmation e-mail manually. Please follow the steps below to do it:

1. Open a contact that has to receive a privacy confirmation e-mail, then click on the Task button and **Send privacy confirmation e-mail.**

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6370114-tSTar0zRjjzAaJ6SuhTuGQemwS9CKzbM0nayMAudWRM55n3bPeZ8O9ATT2HioTiq-0/image-20251020192505-12.png)
2. Then, in the field **Use template** **(1),**you can choose the privacy confirmation template that has to be used, select the **Language (2)**of the template, and change the **Subject** if you prefer. Click **Send,**and a privacy confirmation e-mail will be sent out.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6370115-olIUtAyM3RKcJySNxxz03D6QWehXWhybSoHlSC3gLblGMnjMDIUlMck8DtlS0FpY-0/image-20251020192808-13.png)
