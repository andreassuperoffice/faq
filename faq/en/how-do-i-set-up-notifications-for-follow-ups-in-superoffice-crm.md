# How do I set up notifications for follow-ups in SuperOffice CRM?

Updated on 11 Jun 2025

I want to be reminded about the follow-ups that I have created in my SuperOffice-diary. How do I set up the reminders?

To be notified (get reminders) about upcoming follow-up activities registered in your SuperOffice CRM diary you should:

- Have SuperOffice WebTools is installed and configured. [This FAQ](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-download-and-install-webtools-for-superoffice-crm/) explains how to do it.
- Set up a reminder on the follow-up when you create a follow-up.

Please follow the steps below to create a follow-up with a reminder:

1. Start by creating a follow-up. You can either click on **New** and then **Meeting (1)** or **Follow-up** **(2)** or the **+ Add** **(3)** button on the Activities tab.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6235269-25h9qnp8iFWI7w0QOzQ25Cy3JXBkBycPWuFF0gAlBDP7zH1ccuQsiqnwWAdyooeu-0/image-20250611125204-2.png)
2. Then click on the **Alarm** field and select how many minutes before the appointment the reminder should appear. You can also choose a custom alarm by clicking on **Other** - the maximum value allowed is 1439 minutes.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6235273-KYj81rEAGjt6sWKJXrwlMOcxp7LaMwpUyUaAjMRhlq8fUtdmZJO7SSTqF6BTMSPT-0/image-20250611125636-3.png)

**Please be aware that:**

1. You can configure SuperOffice CRM to automatically set an alarm for follow-ups with your preferred timing. This can be done in **Personal Preferences** under the **Diary** section (1):

![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6235328-ra4nhw6OWLAkgj1gZMb8FEzGiqZ5XSJEOOKgD1pYB8GBS9C7EpC89hXalF083Rda-0/image-20250611131615-6.png)

- Use the **Alarm on follow-up** option (2) to choose whether alarms should be added to follow-ups by default. Select **Yes** to enable alarms, or **No** to disable them.
- Set the **Alarm default time** (3) to define how many minutes before the follow-up the alarm should appear.
- Use the **Silent after** setting (4) to specify how long the alarm should sound before it goes silent.
- Alternatively, you can configure the alarm time in the **WebTools Preferences**dialog by checking the box next to *"Show alarm for all appointments ... minutes before"* and entering the number of minutes.

  ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6235300-hrxE8lcrGDHgNs7auCEZIhU1P5s9UgIotJa15Mh8nH960LC8RSL4NrSOm6eiR7ne-0/image-20250611131304-5.png)

2. When the time for the reminder arrives, an **alarm dialog** opens showing key details such as the **company**, **date**, **time**, **type**, **priority**, and **location** related to the follow-up.The dialog includes the following options:

- **OK (1)** – Dismisses the reminder
- **Show (2)** – Opens the full follow-up activity in SuperOffice CRM for more details or editing.
- **Remind me in 5 minutes (3)** – Snoozes the alarm and reminds you again in 5 minutes.

  ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6235566-70YWsj0BdxOSGyxhh44OFT2Z80kqIBgQ4ELj7TiOadymecumYsWhYBedTsD5kdlL-0/image-20250611143900-7.png)

This allows you to quickly decide whether to act on the appointment now, review more information, or be reminded later.

3. For alarms (reminders) to work in a different time zone than your default time, [the time zone setting must be turned on](https://docs.superoffice.com/en/admin/options/learn/time-zones/enable.html).
