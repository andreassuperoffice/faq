# How do I get a list of all my companies or contacts in SuperOffice CRM?

Updated on 22 Aug 2025

I want to see all my companies and contact persons in one list. How do I do this in SuperOffice CRM?

By using selections, you can easily make actionable lists of all your companies and contacts in SuperOffice CRM. In the examples below, we explain how you can do it.

- [Make a list of all your companies](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-get-a-list-of-all-my-companies-or-contacts-in-superoffice-crm/#1)
- [Make a list of all your contacts](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-get-a-list-of-all-my-companies-or-contacts-in-superoffice-crm/#2)

**Make a list of all your companies**

To do so, please follow the steps below:

1. Click on the **Find**(1) button and then **Find Company**(2).

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6299813-aUJxYOON587bzN6RFwwRNL8TnqXrOz5e7Sag3elqXaCQF25GZP44tXK1rCUFRa3M-0/image-20250822150606-1.png)
2. Next, in the criteria field, click the arrow, which will unfold the list of possible criteria for you to use. Choose **Our contact - Current user (1)**if you want to list your own companies, and click **Find (2).

   Note!** Make sure you selected **Our Contact** from ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6299816-ODT4p3CQ2hNn32RGiSauCu7nSZyjSMK5oCSNTQFN8U869bUwE0RdiBor7rXQ5uL9-0/image-20250822151217-4.png) the **Company,**not ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6299817-SE49rN0j0MyClKcPIHTegy9f3Wqa3zmhPAZnRgkJ5NBwMOgyufFCaifo7qNSQN0X-0/image-20250822151233-5.png) the **Contact** criteria list.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6299814-e6QlAcGxPOlm7ibiOfXCTw7QJqMUqQbhgzdJNXi3S1i013EAXM1MxhGumI4aeHPo-0/image-20250822151121-2.png)
3. You can save the list that you've made [as a selection](https://docs.superoffice.com/en/search-options/selection/learn/create.html). To see more information about companies, you can [set up more columns](https://docs.superoffice.com/en/learn/section-tabs/configure-columns.html). Click on the **Task** button and choose from different actions on this list; e.g., [create a follow-up](https://docs.superoffice.com/en/search-options/selection/learn/howto/generate-follow-ups.html), [create a mailing](https://docs.superoffice.com/en/search-options/selection/learn/howto/create-mailings.html), [bulk update](https://docs.superoffice.com/en/learn/basics/bulk-update.html), [visualize it (create graphs)](https://docs.superoffice.com/en/search-options/selection/learn/howto/display-as-charts.html), etc.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6299830-ObQf72x3hzL8SYwGLpCZkd3KTjzSsofsZkMSOLyziUE0Z50fc6e9lGEZFPg1TO47-0/image-20250822151841-6.png)

**Make a list of all your contacts**

To do so, please follow the steps below:

1. Click on the **Find**(1) button and then **Find Contact**(2).

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6299831-3NYnExeaTRdKDi7985leXsUZSII2tAndsOQz797wfdM9Ub7uYuesn66RQSmZGmQj-0/image-20250822151935-7.png)
2. Next, in the criteria field, click the arrow, which will unfold the list of possible criteria for you to use. Choose **Our contact - Current user (1)**if you want to list your own companies, and click **Find (2).

   Note!** Make sure you selected **Our Contact** from ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6299833-48MOrzLYAleNoleehpiqXLyqhfY9mYXRmN58lOaToLh874yXrL5dHYfhL47TNHJR-0/image-20250822152116-8.png) the **Contact,**not ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6299834-mfmWfD1n9DpwYBqkfxYRfoGegOFpb3OnNUYWMU8hmYC13KpP6OLUHXelaqmUQ1gi-0/image-20250822152132-9.png) the **Company** criteria list.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6299838-R1JJILuyqmDolqHsPwJiR9wJJbcT4J4Rfy8XTSWmfDBm704hcmXaXwFCzw34wBaH-0/image-20250822152319-10.png)
3. You can save the list that you've made as a selection. To see more information about companies, you can set up more columns. Click on the **Task** button and choose from different actions on this list; e.g. [create a follow-up](https://docs.superoffice.com/en/search-options/selection/learn/howto/generate-follow-ups.html), [create a mailing](https://docs.superoffice.com/en/search-options/selection/learn/howto/create-mailings.html), [bulk update](https://docs.superoffice.com/en/learn/basics/bulk-update.html), [visualize it (create graphs)](https://docs.superoffice.com/en/search-options/selection/learn/howto/display-as-charts.html), etc.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6299839-7H1ycN3MQAheU4MtIwjsM0muwA6Y5st3bN8q3yFjurSuY4tFohIJDbwUClGoCaMM-0/image-20250822152452-11.png)
