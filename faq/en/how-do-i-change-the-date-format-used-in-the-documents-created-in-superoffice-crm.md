# How do I change the date format used in the documents created in SuperOffice CRM?

Updated on 24 Jul 2025

My document template uses the long and short date variables {date} {sdat}. How do I change the format of the date filled in by these variables?

![](https://online2.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/4406199-XxDqX0gDg0FXZp9PPDXQFuEZJmLYtlOagIU94M0wmxO9ntB6QB8lwo87XEXuhPCU-0/image.png)

The format of these variables is determined by the date format on the computer where the document is being created. To change the date format in your SuperOffice documents, you need to change the date format on your computer. Please check [this article](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/answers/questions/3976567/how-do-i-change-the-date-and-time-format-in-window) on how it is done.

**Note!** The date format in SuperOffice CRM can be changed by going to ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6275643-bc344tYQeSbiQxZxQSRwniTIrQG1wyhBFEMEkFoNkzoMBIFGxHQPRxzG5r20A2P8-0/image-20250724161201-2.png) then choose **Local settings**, and the **Number and date format** field.
