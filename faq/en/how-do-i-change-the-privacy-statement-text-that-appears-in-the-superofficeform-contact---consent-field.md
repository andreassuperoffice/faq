# How do I change the Privacy statement text that appears in the SuperOffice form "Contact - Consent" field?

Updated on 28 Dec 2025

A privacy confirmation statement appears when a customer clicks on the Information icon in a SuperOffice form. How do I change this text?

![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6465329-reOs7tanrcuPw5nKrCq8xcPjneCckSwh6gFEPFbfS0Gg1MayYCTyCpY4D1TsQADN-0/image-20251228101945-5.png)

To change the text, which is displayed in the form when a customer clicks on the information button, please follow the steps below:

1. Open the form that has the privacy confirmation text that you want to change. Click on **Marketing (1),** then **Forms (2),**and in Recent, My forms, Active forms, or All forms tabs, find your form and double-click on it (3).

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6465326-uWXxsDcZKalhR5cq6kKYj485gwiTmmIH6WjfMq64XOnEfBFJbbFEJm199e9URJA4-0/image-20251228101144-2.png)
2. Determine which type of consent is used in the form. To do it, open the Edit form screen, click on the **Fields (1)**section, then find and expand the "Contact - Consent" field, and in the **Purpose (2)** field, you will see which consent is used.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6465325-mnQGoH3ZyGMfClFqti6gBTFSyjWAwX6xRaaGDLZJMMCYkkA7g7YY6yJxghxSpl91-0/image-20251228100721-1.png)
3. Login [Settings and maintenance](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-access-administration-part-settings--maintenance-in-superoffice-crm/), click on the **Privacy (1)**button, and double-click on the consent purpose (2), which is used in the form.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6465327-uRXstt4Juq2c3BZjxCn5acU3j26bKOYiSmQY7CfbUnP0LEx2MpdpQj58IMWqRQ3W-0/image-20251228101513-3.png)
4. The field **External description in Customer Center (1)** stores a text, which is displayed after clicking on the information button in the form. You can change it there and click **Save (2)** to store changes.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6465328-ZM4dPFiAzK9c3ZkSK5IqX53iMsW5NA33brKms6LLTRRkiOTlibtRLDuwoLaTMdq1-0/image-20251228101619-4.png)
