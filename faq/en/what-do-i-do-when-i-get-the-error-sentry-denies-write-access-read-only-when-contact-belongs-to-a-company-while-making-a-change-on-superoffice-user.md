# What do I do when I get the error “Sentry denies Write access: Read-Only when contact belongs to a company” while making a change on SuperOffice user?

Updated on 28 May 2025

I try to remove a license from a user, but I get the error message saying “Sentry denies Write access: Read-Only when contact belongs to a company.” Why does it happen and how do I fix it?

![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6223061-nj8GbXJOqjpd5G77ton763FY3lgS83cJS5D01oMjt51kisAIclM9t3IbSZnIMMSk-0/image-20250528150226-1.png)

Sentry errors are often related to not having enough data rights to make a change on a record.

Also another known situations when this error may pop up is when **Our Contact, Category** or **Business** field is empty on [your company card](https://online2.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi?_sf=0&custSessionKey=&customerLang=en&noCookies=true&action=viewKbEntry&id=112970).

![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6223077-1o1tho1wuI0qXRWftu2SHGIfIzWKxC7iaR2pmE3y7ZXKWWT8CP2tghnPwjy5dTfg-0/image-20250528151416-2.png)

**Note!** If your SuperOffice CRM has more than one [own company](https://docs.superoffice.com/en/admin/user-management/learn/our-companies.html) in the same system, please make sure that you select a correct company and confirm that all the above mentioned fields are filled in.
