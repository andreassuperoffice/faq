# How do I find a SuperOffice CRM serial number?

Updated on 24 Jul 2025

How do I find a SuperOffice CRM serial number?

To find your SuperOffice installation serial number, please follow the section that explains where it is stored in the type of SuperOffice client that you use:

- [If you use SuperOffice Web and Online browser application](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-find-a-superoffice-crm-serial-number/#2)
- [If you use SuperOffice Windows application](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-find-a-superoffice-crm-serial-number/#1)

​

**SuperOffice Web and Online browser application**

To find your serial number, please follow the steps below:

1. Log in to [Settings and Maintenance](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-access-administration-part-settings--maintenance-in-superoffice-crm/).
2. Click on **Licences**and in the Status tab you will see your number next to the line **Serial number.**

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6275600-E1gzmqCRCOU23Lki7Us6G9smto6dgp8ZWT7djeOStYqBXn4G6oSgM6PnXD9BNnzb-0/image-20250724154057-1.png)

**Note!** SuperOffice Online customers have the serial numbers, but another important indicator about your installation is customer ID. You can know it by looking into your SuperOffice CRM URL. The numbers next to Cust are your customer ID.

![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6275601-j2l1NHaOrbulGKqpF4a4dExUI9OYzwYUHF3HXDFADXNuISfJTzQ1IwISptaEzhdg-0/image-20250724154210-2.png)

​

**SuperOffice Windows application**

If you use SuperOffice 8 Windows application, you will find a serial number under the **Help** button in the **About** section.
