# How do I create and edit a video meeting invitation e-mail template in SuperOffice CRM?

Updated on 31 Mar 2026

How do I create and edit a video meeting invitation e-mail template in SuperOffice CRM?

In SuperOffice, predefined e-mail invitations are sent when a person is invited to a video meeting. You can edit the video meeting invitation e-mail templates in [Settings and Maintenance](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-access-administration-part-settings--maintenance-in-superoffice-crm/). Please follow the steps below to edit templates:

1. Click on **Lists** (1), select **E-mail template** option from the drop-down list, and in the list double-click the template, which you want to edit (3).

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6611244-3nxxWNlFCHEbSgJWGE4h3NEDYtXZW02gco34hsrRi9t9WLHy9mP9qGx7I7lUeCEi-0/image-20260331105540-1.png)
   - The New meeting template is used when you create a new appointment.
   - The Changed meeting template is used when you make time changes to an already existing appointment.
   - The Cancelled meeting template is used when you delete an already existing appointment.

     **Note!** The text of these templates will be used only if you select the option enabling sending e-mail invitations to the appointment participant.

     ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6611280-tjT2JXs3Fqqq3U2jiKfGBgOdauR2xCpocWr2eAkLJmy9LkKiJokEQ3wOHbxEmFJP-0/image-20260331110354-2.png)

     You can also choose to send e-mail invitations to attendees for all meetings by default in personal preferences:

     ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6612414-FemQx1dsXP8KcialynWdWWNAje84eDOcfGw5eBg7jNdTIsQWK4PBP6aJaZnMllqD-0/image-20260331164513-1.png)
2. Click **Edit** to open a template editor.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6611325-AJbwgnTRDLEeRQsk7JJ47481Y2wO9TUajjkmpE5BEu5OjK4UUhaCMjwM59nrxkfk-0/image-20260331111220-3.png)
3. Edit the invitation text. You can use [all available template variables](https://docs.superoffice.com/en/document/templates/learn/template-variables.html). The variable {burl} (1) inserts the link to your video meeting. When you are happy with the changes, click **Save** (2).

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6611367-rkteYEOzOP5Wkc7PAWIeUizAklGxyjPL9RYww7L2FInd3t09C3dXZp6hyC4gos8c-0/image-20260331111605-4.png)

**Note!**

1. To be able to send video meeting invitations and see received external invitations in your diary, you must have either [SuperOffice Inbox](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-start-using-the-superoffice-inbox/) or [SuperOffice MailLink](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-configure-superoffice-maillink-for-outlook-manually/) configured.
2. If you use SuperOffice MailLink to receive external invitations to video meetings, you must have **Add / update appointment in SuperOffice when responding to invitations in Outlook** (2), option selected in **Invitations** (1) section.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6612430-RLJJ9jQbYiUO4SI7ZBolf09q3XwqpO2SXmhOnuELLyEdL71w0zX4XLoMNfB4W7yB-0/image-20260331164902-2.png)
