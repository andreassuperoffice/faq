# Why do I get a browser pop-up when trying to open a document/email in SuperOffice CRM?

Updated on 15 Dec 2023

Why do I get a browser pop-up when trying to open a document/email in SuperOffice CRM?

SuperOffice uses the program called WebTools to download and open documents on your computer.

Starting from SuperOffice CRM version 10.2.11, WebTools has changed some key components in how the system fetches the document.

To access our WebTools functionality from the browser, you would be asked for permission to open the different components needed for handling documents and emails.

This dialog will show up the first time you try to create or open a document in SuperOffice CRM. The dialog will vary depending on the browser.
