# How can I restore companies, contacts, or requests deleted from SuperOffice?

Updated on 25 Jul 2025

I deleted some companies, contacts, or requests from SuperOffice CRM. How do I restore the deleted records?

If companies, contacts, or requests have been deleted less than 14 days ago, you can restore them from the **Recycle bin**.

To restore deleted records, please follow the steps below:

1. Click on ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6275793-PC5Fw7UA37rJdur1hUfCHS5pqpmBgJtblrjgppcwrdGpTSz1tqq5t5tmK5PDmeKO-0/image-20250725080546-1.png) then select **Recycle bin** from the drop-down menu.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6275794-4tLXy1PEG557L7GZCSQaRHfm5QCXn6OBQeiGadm2dAk5hAsGhQJZx7ruZiHBP1x4-0/image-20250725080558-2.png)
2. Select the records that you want to restore from the list (1) and click **Restore (2).**In the same way, you can restore deleted contacts - just click on the **Contact** tab. To restore deleted requests, click on the **Requests** tab.

   **Note!** The option to **View deleted companies/contacts for all users (3)** will be visible, only if you have the [functional right](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/whichare-all-availablefunctional-rights-in-superoffice-and-what-each-of-them-mean/) of a **General administrator**.
3. Click **OK (4)**, to close the screen.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6275795-a0LQsuwxfT6IcwmaO9XCHiW8b8P3G37qXdqRM8zykJaS154E14wm4h3maIBPMUGy-0/image-20250725080831-3.png)

   **Important!**

- If you have deleted a company, all the connected contacts will also be deleted.
- If you then restore the company again, all the contacts will also be restored.
- If you only restore one of the contacts on the deleted company, the company + only the selected contact will be restored, not all of the contacts.
- If you delete a contact without any company linked, then only the contact will be restored.
- To restore deleted requests, you must have the [functional right](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/whichare-all-availablefunctional-rights-in-superoffice-and-what-each-of-them-mean/) **Edit all requests**. To delete requests, your role must include **Service administration** or **General Administrator**.

**Note!**

1. All deleted records will disappears from Recycle bin in 14 days or another number of days that has been defined in Settings and Maintenance **Preferences** (1), **Global preferences** (2), **Retain deleted data** (3).

![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6275806-0PkQn6wHPUBBpT9T9LylbpUWPGhao7recQGNFe1efxmj9YHLdbsqRqKBSxBakSX3-0/image-20250725081401-4.png)

2. If you need to restore companies, contacts, requests, or other types of data after they are permanently deleted, we recommend contacting a SuperOffice consultant. Please note that this is a paid service, and the cost depends on the type and amount of data to be restored. If you use a SuperOffice Onsite solution, data can only be restored if you can provide a database backup containing the deleted data. If you use SuperOffice Online, please contact SuperOffice support to request a database backup.
