# What to do when I get a notification that our licence information cannot be updated automatically?

Updated on 1 Apr 2026

When I log in, I get a notification stating that the SuperOffice licence will soon reach its expiry date.

![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6614688-xzYQo8mJZ7eK1OioT2KJO86n5Y19dc1aw2Rz68dNP26WBU3Pq8S42qCEzMnGsGIt-0/image-20260401153142-3.png)

Why do I get this message, and how can I remove it?

The most common reasons why customers are getting a notification that the licence cannot be updated automatically are:

- [More user licences are assigned to users than have been bought.](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/what-to-do-when-i-get-a-notification-that-our-licence-information-cannot-be-updated-automatically/#1)
- [A customer tries to log in to the wrong installation.](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/what-to-do-when-i-get-a-notification-that-our-licence-information-cannot-be-updated-automatically/#2)
- [We haven't received a customer's payment for the licences yet.](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/what-to-do-when-i-get-a-notification-that-our-licence-information-cannot-be-updated-automatically/#3)
- [[ONSITE] The licence in the SuperOffice licence system is different than the actual one on your server](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/what-to-do-when-i-get-a-notification-that-our-licence-information-cannot-be-updated-automatically/#4)

​

**More user licences are assigned to users than have been bought.**

This usually happens when a customer reduces the number of licences in the contract, but the excess licences are not removed from users. To be able to fetch the new licence and remove the notification message, you should manually remove the licence that you no longer have access to.
For example, if you have 10 Sales Standard licenses and will change them to Sales Premium licenses, you have to manually remove the 10 Sales Standard licenses from the users who use them before you can fetch the new Sales Premium licenses.

There are two ways to do this.

[**1. Use the Remove excess use of licenses function.**](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/what-to-do-when-i-get-a-notification-that-our-licence-information-cannot-be-updated-automatically/#5)

**[2. Manually remove excess licenses from the users.](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/what-to-do-when-i-get-a-notification-that-our-licence-information-cannot-be-updated-automatically/#6)**

**1. Use the Remove excess use of licenses function.**

1. Click on **Remove excess use of licence before activating the new license.**

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6614682-Mm6jQh0Zjk6kSmO68coDoJgm7wjCLXT9wfd8448Z4hAC5p9jYlhe9fB9nCAKrNFY-0/image-20260401152819-2.png)
2. In the dialogue, you will see which licences should be removed. Click on a user plan under **User licences,**which is in red.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6614689-7Aj8XOIquiWBPladHXX65FuaNL3YLlf5ebAJuEYsp1yxL9PtE2l2doh9SzqnEZ87-0/image-20260401153527-4.png)
3. Select a user from the list (1) and click **Reassign** (2) if you have a license to reassign to the user. Click **Delete**(3) if you want to remove the license from the user. Repeat this procedure if more users have excess licences and continue until all of them are removed, and then click **OK** (4).

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6614802-qCzNmJD0AC6H8urgJMgog7Z7CrYKhMdp8exLgIUquOJesNcHaOOwJObDntl6kg2G-0/image-20260401161655-7.png)
4. You'll be redirected back to the activation screen. Click on **Activate**to initiate licence activation.
5. When the licence is activated successfully, you'll see a text notifying you that the **new licence has been activated.**

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6614813-rfMfStIkUZaU8Yu3oS0Xwx5HSyJeUh7Bw6NsrRPrEJfYgklqRpWzwSYGuhzDrYCh-0/image-20260401162114-8.png)
6. When you have received new licenses, you can now assign them to the users again.

**2. Manually remove excess licenses from the users.**

1. Go to the **User tab** in Settings and Maintenance and click on a user who is using a license that is not in your license plan anymore.
2. Select '(No Selection)' in the **User Plan** field and click **Save**.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6614816-0huWB5jmbWFuRz0Ikh6UqxzqTUZ4mUUtYDCALFZODQhJg4RwzwKImK0izBlM9waE-0/image-20260401162335-9.png)
3. Repeat these steps for each user who is using a license you no longer have access to.
4. Go to the **Licenses tab**, click on the **SuperOffice** tab and click on '**Click here to fetch your new license information from the web**'.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6614832-RIgH6iFi5PcH3XHcqNzBgto9fBWarAlLX0ydGFQsLaH4BTtX2mQc1JaMyYmyDiM6-0/image-20260401162647-12.png)
5. When the licence is activated successfully, you'll see a text notifying you that the new**licence has been activated.**

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6614813-rfMfStIkUZaU8Yu3oS0Xwx5HSyJeUh7Bw6NsrRPrEJfYgklqRpWzwSYGuhzDrYCh-0/image-20260401162114-8.png)
6. When you have received the new licenses, you can now assign them to the users again.

**Note!**If you get an error: *"The user has open requests in Service. The licence cannot be removed,"*read [this FAQ](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/what-to-do-when-i-get-an-error-the-user-could-not-be-changed-for-the-following-reasons-open-or-postponed-requests-while-trying-to-remove-access-to-superoffice-crm/).

​

**A customer tries to log in to the wrong installation.**

All new customers have the option to continue working on their free trial installation. However, they can get an empty one once their free trial licence expires. Sometimes, users try to log in to the free trial installation instead of the new production installation.

If this situation sounds familiar, please make sure that you are logging into the correct installation. Contact your SuperOffice administrator and confirm the CustID and the Serial number of your production installation. You can see the name and licence number of your SuperOffice by clicking on **Help**and **About.**[This FAQ](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-find-a-superoffice-crm-serial-number/) also explains how to see it.

​

**We haven't received a customer's payment for the licences yet.**

If there has been a delay or issue with the payment for licences, the system won't update the licence automatically.

Please confirm that your company has made a successful payment to SuperOffice. If you have confirmed the payment and none of the situations described in this FAQ help you to update the licence, please [contact SuperOffice support](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-register-a-support-request-from-within-superoffice-crm/) for help.

​

**[ONSITE] The licence in the SuperOffice licence system is different than the actual one on your server.**

This situation can happen when a SuperOffice customer upgrades their SuperOffice installation to a version for which they don't have a maintenance contract. E.g. They have a maintenance contract for version 8, but they upgraded to version 9.

The solution, in this case, is to [contact SuperOffice support](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-register-a-support-request-from-within-superoffice-crm/) or your contact person in SuperOffice to get help.
