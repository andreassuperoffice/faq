# What to do when I cannot login to SuperOffice CRM Online?

Updated on 30 Apr 2026

What to do when I cannot login to SuperOffice CRM Online?

There could be several reasons why you are not able to log into your SuperOffice CRM Online. There are some troubleshooting steps that you can do to help yourself, and they depend on whether you were able to log in before or if you have never logged into SuperOffice CRM. Please follow the instructions in one of the sections below:

- [If you have never logged in before, please click here](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/what-to-do-when-i-cannot-login-to-superoffice-crm-online/#1)
- [If you have logged in before, please click here](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/what-to-do-when-i-cannot-login-to-superoffice-crm-online/#2)

​

**If you have never logged in before**

Please contact your SuperOffice administration and ask to confirm that:

- When your [user was created](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-create-a-new-user-in-superoffice-crm/), the user plan was selected.

  ![](https://app.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6657787-NCwREiYqlDdrpKW7RQ1e6rYRIRL5tqfxWcgdqwuwp9UzKrKXmk0ZOUQJ6PgYHgiK-0/image-20260430131025-1.png)
- Your contact card has the same email address as your user card in Settings and Maintenance.

  ![](https://app.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6657808-BjHZqqMaU8u9Zf1I8JeGY6yFReCdjXKALdF2CURUpk34oaRtDm1w8NR0R7b40Sbq-0/image-20260430131200-2.png)
- If your SuperOffice installation uses SuperID authentication, check your inbox for an invitation email. You need to click the activation link to activate the SuperOffice user. If you cannot find the e-mail in the main box, check your Spam folder or ask your administrator to resend the welcome e-mail To do it, the administrator needs to log in to [Settings and Maintenance](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-access-administration-part-settings--maintenance-in-superoffice-crm/), click on the **Users** (1) button, select your user (2), click on the **Task** (3)button, and choose the **Send welcome email** (4)option.

**If you were able to log in before following this section**

1. First of all, make sure you did not make any spelling mistakes, CapsLock is not On and NumLock is on if you used numbers in your password.
2. Try to log in to SuperOffice Online using a different browser. If you can log in on different browsers, you need to delete the cookies of the browser you usually use. Read [this article](https://www.digitaltrends.com/computing/how-to-delete-cookies/) to learn how to delete cookies on the most popular browsers.
3. Sometimes the login issue can be caused by the fact that your contact has more than one email address in the contact card. Try to delete the email address you do NOT use to log in.
4. If you forgot your password, you can click on the hyperlink in the login screen **Forgot the password?**

If neither of these solutions helps, please register a request on the SuperOffice [Support portal](http://cs.superoffice.com).
