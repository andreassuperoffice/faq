# How do I configure SuperOffice MailLink for Outlook manually?

Updated on 30 Jul 2026

I am not able to archive or click on Inbox in SuperOffice and get my Outlook application opened. I have WebTools and MailLink installed. Why does it happen and how do I fix it?

If you experienced changes in your computer environment (OS or browser updates, new browser, new computer, etc.), it can cause the MailLink configuration to be lost.

First of all, make sure that both **MS Office (Outlook)** and **SuperOffice WebTools** are installed locally on the computer. You should find both of these applications in the Control Panel, Programs and Features section.

If you have both WebTools and Outlook installed, you can configure MailLink manually by following these two steps:

[Step 1. Set the configuration in SuperOffice CRM](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-configure-superoffice-maillink-for-outlook-manually/#1)

[Step 2. Set configuration in Outlook](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-configure-superoffice-maillink-for-outlook-manually/#2)

**Step 1: Set the configuration in SuperOffice CRM**

1. Click on the **Personal Settings** iconin the top right corner of the screen and choose **Local Settings** from the drop-down menu.

   ![](https://app.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6798876-Q2wAL66jRcQCgD4NrSfSMgE93q6OhH2obNGkwnQWy5uNmPCOYtkADbU0x1GqD0wk-0/image-20260730135550-1.png)
2. Choose **Microsoft Outlook** as an **E-mail client** and click **OK.**

   ![](https://app.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6798877-lNktddWmKG0lXyclwKA8iU270Znp4nWirICGq6uFwEdEMK2RKfoCSTfuQgHSd9Hw-0/image-20260730135652-2.png)

   **Note!**Make sure that SuperOffice WebTools are installed on your computer by navigating to Control Panel > Programs and Features. Otherwise**,** you'll have to [install WebTools first](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-download-and-install-webtools-for-superoffice-crm/).

   ![](https://app.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6798902-4pyQ7L9AfDG6hSZ7OT9DqlRZXNnQZcUgWxPeHTOGCK5K0ihIBO2TWwIYJDIB1csi-0/image-20260730140658-1.png)

**Step 2: Set a configuration in Outlook**

1. Click on the arrow in the SuperOffice Ribbon in your Outlook it will open the **Options** dialog.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6468834-mr1MDrIC6OllURJ8lTrGuCOmcx45mJow1foY0KHCP1ck5MCQME4N0s1QneLpuWRY-0/image-20260105133559-7.png)
2. In **General settings**, click on thearrow ![](http://content.screencast.com/users/mrfrodeberntsen/folders/Snagit/media/7fe83d1d-68c9-4fb9-8fe3-206f687c01da/03.26.2013-09.47.png) next to **Advanced settings,**and in the **URL for SuperOffice CRM** field, copy your SuperOffice CRM URL address**.**

   ![](https://app.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6798882-P71UggU6Edq94LKCSyaaOyIY5iifSOCfiWf2hIKLFBGj74w6erWH4fnI306TQ6Pi-0/image-20260730135940-5.png)
3. In the field **User name (1),** type in your user name and click on the **Test connection (2)** button.

   ![](https://app.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6798885-joJkpNDe7whPGWOSKLp5C7oPcf81AEwyYrQz1WZfHPEoWd4FRI43YSc0T1qtORPH-0/image-20260730140324-6.png)
4. If you use SuperOffice Online, you'll be asked to get authenticated via the browser; click **OK** to do it.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6166306-ogGgpWwAO0b0lCJ7Jnl1etKqaGIpl3waYl6exmaoc7DIJpSdF1F66chn1mG9S5q5-0/image-20250407201358-7.png)

   If you use SuperOffice Onsite, in the MailLink login dialog, enter your SuperOffice user name and password and click on the **OK** button.

   ![](https://online2.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/3828724-CuJAmWa9y9gYItxPXb7CyWNWf3aeAw2FCdl1GTaL0ZI1TESmdCGm63FFb2LatfUp-0/image.png)
5. When you see a dialog that confirms that the **Connection succeeded**, click**Close** and **OK** to close open windows.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6166308-ICdhA4qxyZ6isjs2E5I1uYH4t0d0N97Eb1dHThimUW3BmE9CipX5Dt7PUeaZWaj7-0/image-20250407201738-8.png)
6. You might be asked to authenticate via browser again. Click **OK** and then close the window that will open in the browser.
