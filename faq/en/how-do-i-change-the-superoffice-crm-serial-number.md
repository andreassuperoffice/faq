# How do I change the SuperOffice CRM serial number?

Updated on 26 Mar 2026

How do I change our SuperOffice CRM serial number?

To change the serial number, you need to do three steps:

[Step 1. Request a new serial number](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-change-the-superoffice-crm-serial-number/#1)

[Step 2. Change it in your SuperOffice CRM](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-change-the-superoffice-crm-serial-number/#2)

[Step 3. Update licence information](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-change-the-superoffice-crm-serial-number/#3)

​

**Step 1. Request a new serial number**

To get a new serial number, you should contact your account manager in SuperOffice or [register a request](https://community.superoffice.com/nl/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-register-a-support-request-from-within-superoffice-crm/) with SuperOffice support.

​

**Step 2. Change the serial number in your SuperOffice CRM**

After receiving confirmation that you were issued a new serial number, you should update the serial number in SuperOffice CRM. Please follow the steps below:

1. Log in to [Settings & Maintenance](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-access-administration-part-settings--maintenance-in-superoffice-crm/).
2. Click on ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6607380-ACdRkSa7oK8Xe1rXE7Z1QcWcYHfbCUC9uJRgwMoiOPgBcdCesXqrN60wLmnqYIii-0/image-20260326215500-2.png) and **Change serial number.**

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6607379-YHsDYru9dmdEzDPrNjB6DJ8t6fZMOUwc5Hi5urlLKZfYmBCYh5dc0ryCbZLXNq4u-0/image-20260326215430-1.png)
3. In the dialog, enter **New serial number** (1), which you received from SuperOffice, and **New licence owner** (2) - company's name, and click **OK**(3)**.**

![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6607381-x7xQUuBaExLo1U6pmA5kyodSONL5DHq83H9FFCCRFF3WH0j0xkCsNbBa2oAx5Q2f-0/image-20260326215732-3.png)

​

**Step 3. Update licence information**

If you use SuperOffice 8.x licence information is going to be updated automatically via the internet.

If you use SuperOffice 7.x licence information needs to be updated manually. Check that you have the correct number of licences for the module(s) in question in the +/- column. An increase in the number of licences is shown in green. A decrease is shown in red. If you have the correct number of licences for the modules you require, click the **Activate button**.
