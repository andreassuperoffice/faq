# What to do when I get an error "You do not have access to view this request" when trying to access a request in SuperOffice Service?

Updated on 24 Feb 2026

I want to open a request in SuperOffice Service, but I get a message saying "*Request cannot be viewed*". Why does it happen, and how do I fix it?

![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6552938-zUKqq7B9cssR2jQWYeYa8KLmsdSlrWdW1BIGk8o03eF3hixaDEhL6Q6edm3e7Gab-0/image-20260224133256-1.png)

This message appears when the category where the request is stored is accessible only to category members.

To assign the user to the category members, please follow the steps below:

1. Click on the ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6552946-sdCHx1BYM6eAEjPaZwFeXSuLE30tu9Ak7tqq3Kz7d4032lSBsNGEIzAjRIgfaizg-0/image-20260224133442-3.png) button, and then **Settings and Maintenance**.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6552945-thirf5tQUnHqPbcFqCuQBajCUxEHSOr3R5K3IQRluPMY3MZ9bTeFctXsNOPMTFMp-0/image-20260224133419-2.png)
2. Then navigate to **Requests** - **Categories,**and click on the category where the request that cannot be accessed is stored.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6552974-UqDQkeU3Thmpsjl1Hs1909yR12symheCsDxb4dzAm3hSgF9Ic01irqsyMpmufCEp-0/image-20260224133945-4.png)
3. At the bottom of the **Members (1)**tab, select the user who cannot see the request (2), and click the **+Add** (3) button.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6552986-mkAwoRak2qYrdMz06yPWr1P2DaJqDsLpuMTWMMqutnDC8hf036MTyaey8XQ1e3jw-0/image-20260224134241-5.png)

   Alternatively, you can choose to remove the setting limiting access to the requests stored in the category. To do so, open the category, click on the **Properties (1)**tab, deselect the tick next to **Only members have access (2)**, and click **OK (3)** to save the setting.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6552988-2qtSHPkMzi5jY9n9MpEqJU8UbQk0yYXihOhUQM7x5UT9jQnFo1ppccjRFa8UuMKi-0/image-20260224134502-6.png)
