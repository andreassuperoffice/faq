# How do I change a currency rate in SuperOffice CRM?

Updated on 12 Jun 2025

The currency conversion doesn't count the correct values as the rate has changed. Where do I change a currency rate in SuperOffice CRM?

To change a currency rate in SuperOffice CRM please follow the steps below:

1. Login to SuperOffice [Settings and Maintenance](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-access-administration-part-settings--maintenance-in-superoffice-crm/).
2. Click on the **Lists** (1) button and select **General - Currency** (2)from the top drop-down menu.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6237622-dFBp4zBmSlN3Kbayz4L2uRnxZqUhsQWfrcbHGHSSF7fzgdbO8JpBOS74TDQH2tcg-0/image-20250612152446-1.png)
3. In the currency, list find the currency, which you want to update and double-click on it.
4. A new window will appear where you can make changes and insert a new **Rate (1)** number, click **Save (2)** to finish.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6237632-EQca4UtpinnI6MFYrH55HSOpSW3p3TS488DadjZOrPq5hiijEsIupDhDL2fkpraL-0/image-20250612152731-2.png)

**Note!** This is the rate against the base currency. The base currency is the currency, which has a rate of 1 to 1. In the example below, the base currency is Euro. The rate for the other currencies is calculated against this currency in terms of the specified unit. This might mean for example that GBP 1 corresponds to EUR 1.562 at the day’s rate, while NOK 100 would correspond to EUR 12.3677.
