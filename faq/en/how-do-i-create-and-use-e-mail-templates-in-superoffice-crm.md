# How do I create and use e-mail templates in SuperOffice CRM?

Updated on 30 Jul 2026

What is the way to create an e-mail template in SuperOffice CRM and use it when I am sending e-mails?

In SuperOffice CRM, you can easily create your personalized e-mail templates and use them to send e-mails to your customers. Please follow the steps below to do it:

[Step 1. Create an e-mail template in SuperOffice CRM](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-create-and-use-e-mail-templates-in-superoffice-crm/#1)

[Step 2. Use your e-mail template](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-create-and-use-e-mail-templates-in-superoffice-crm/#2)

​

**Step 1. Create an e-mail template in SuperOffice CRM**

Please follow the steps below:

1. Log in to [Settings and Maintenance](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-access-administration-part-settings--maintenance-in-superoffice-crm/) in SuperOffice CRM.
2. Click on the **Lists** (1) button in the Navigator panel, then select **E-mail templates** (2) from the drop-down list and click on **+Add** (3).

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6390888-GoesED6FwfetLJQB4pHqNDyH18JI2UP1NxUWyiTmphZM9HqYx4WGzzOIG2E5KuUA-0/image-20251105142523-1.png)
3. Fill in the name for your template (1), choose a type (2) of the template, select **Create a new e-mail template** (3), if you wish to create a template from scratch, and click **Save** (4).

   **Note!** You also have options to upload .htm/.html templates from your computer or copy an existing e-mail template from SuperOffice.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6390891-ntSGDk4O65iPsNSHlJ0xDoJtRIaFS0fU7DO00kmW5c6Mj9mcAStP4B6fi6BkTfKy-0/image-20251105142812-2.png)
4. Click on **Edit** to open the e-mail text editor.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6390895-kOebx5tbsYO4mDPRYMN7yyP1oyKtidwLEcXpI9kfPQbsyFjQdqXR3hBF4DfBDEGy-0/image-20251105143047-3.png)
5. Fill in your e-mail template with your text. In your e-mail templates, you can use variables, which will automatically fill in information from SuperOffice CRM click on the ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6390915-07nELmiQ6PRpbiLzsHWDrSB2gigFwNsW7pRPX4IoXzSpaeeDWFQJ5k4zteAJZdlF-0/image-20251105143313-5.png)  button (1) to get a list of all available variables. When you are done, click on the **Save** (2) button to finalize.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6390916-UcmhpKYKPgKEqqiRdwQKqscuKlLPBLersyEQ0frDHaKTXzwlWmXY5aTf3nXcNUNc-0/image-20251105143424-6.png)
6. Fill in the **Subject** (1) line of your template. You can use template variables in the subject line, but note that the option **Show e-mail dialog** (3) has to be selected for them to work.

   Then add a tick next to **Use signature in new e-mail** (2) if you want your templates to contain your signature. Please note that the template will use your e-mail signature, as defined in SuperOffice CRM.

   Add a tick next to **Show e-mail dialog** (3) if you want to be able to pre-select new e-mail details.

   Next, choose the **Direction** (4) of how this e-mail template should be used: as an Outgoing template or as a template to archive Incoming e-mails.

   Then, select **Intention** (5) of the e-mail. Intention field lists all the intentions that you have defined in the *Follow-up – Intentions* list.

   Choose where the document template should be stored (6).

   Fill in **Our reference** (7) field if you want to use a sequence number of the e-mail template you are using, and click **Save** (8) to finish.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6390930-DlccObkNiYJ2dLoRMEdi6mLACWbWdkzAoEFN0dZHBG7kheC5IgTK9B9OT8KTtRd8-0/image-20251105143825-7.png)

​

**Step 2. Use your e-mail templates**

There are three ways you can use your e-mail templates:

- [When a new document dialog is in use](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-create-and-use-e-mail-templates-in-superoffice-crm/#1.1)
- [When SuperOffice Inbox is in use](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-create-and-use-e-mail-templates-in-superoffice-crm/#2.2)
- [When Outlook is in use](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-create-and-use-e-mail-templates-in-superoffice-crm/#3.3)

**When a new document dialog is in use**

All SuperOffice users can use e-mail templates by following the steps below:

1. Click on the **+New**button at the top of the screen and then click on **Document**.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6390971-RJKt9CCsPOqckrD0pY76tkxLfP2dJzmIphXw7haIezA0mnjqzEc3zOkBWggCXM72-0/image-20251105144159-8.png)
2. Then choose the e-mail template (1) from the drop-down list and click on the **Create** (2) button.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6390980-GcWeWgQE27GdPx6WiPjBmRoNTqwAsCOGn11gpdb4EfrSTCetRwJ6KSICw4uZkB2T-0/image-20251105144352-9.png)

**When SuperOffice Inbox is in use**

If you use [SuperOffice Inbox](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-start-using-the-superoffice-inbox/) to manage your e-mails, then you can use e-mail templates by following the steps below:

1. Click on the arrow (1) next to the new e-mail button. Choose your e-mail template (2) from the list of all available e-mail templates.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6391068-ukbbQQ5iRirapt2xDqM4K1wpZWAdnuufLpKSXd2XoQOUt4Loy3IDhXSRjiK7yudJ-0/image-20251105150150-12.png)
2. If you enabled the option **Show e-mail dialog** when creating a template, a new e-mail dialog appears before the text is populated to a new e-mail. In the dialog, you can change a template (1), pre-select the company (2) and contact (3), change the e-mail **Subject** (4), pre-select **Sale** (5), and **Project** (6), also make it [Visible for you or your user group](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-make-some-of-my-activities-invisible-from-other-superoffice-users-private/) (7), and select the **Language** (8) of the template text. Click **Create** (9) when you are happy with the setup.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6391078-aXi80R8Hut4B7x6KrIZX1NxdWKsR1xLYswUGUa7KCZtFs7NmkielBEkoH1NdQgTO-0/image-20251105150621-13.png)

**When Outlook is in use**

If you use Outlook to manage your e-mails, then you can use e-mail templates by following the steps below:

1. Click on the S**uperOffice Message button** in the Outlook Ribbon.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6391044-eTpWCUeEAxd1fSRXxnfqlzGFw36E0O1dxRumJyyX7QA8ydxL4yYogpYDL8xBXQbN-0/image-20251105145339-10.png)
2. From the drop-down list, choose your template.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6391055-yo5tQwFy47IUdEUg3zdEFfqq6t8jfUp7WrdziFSZu4cX3nGHllAMSL8sQN1YMNoD-0/image-20251105145421-11.png)
3. If you enabled the option **Show e-mail dialog** when creating a template, a new e-mail dialog will appear before the text is populated to a new e-mail. In this dialog, you can pre-select the company and the contact (1), change the e-mail **Subject** (2), pre-select **Sale** (3) and **Project** (4), select to whom this e-mail will be visible (5), the **Language** (6) of the template text, and even change the template (7). Click **OK** (8) when you are happy with the setup.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6391173-1TecAfpGFrj1s3M5kbQzUuC6ChMLwsuOTplTSSr5W9ppZWo2pCzhhIjSeUn5n2zC-0/image-20251105152523-15.png)
