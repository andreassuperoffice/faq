# How do I create a plain HTML template or import my current HTML template in SuperOffice CRM?

Updated on 26 Nov 2025

I have custom HTML templates created for our company, how do I import them into SuperOffice CRM?

To create a new or import a current HTML template, please follow the steps below:

1. Click on the **Marketing (1)**button and then on the tab **Message Templates (2)**, **All templates (3),** and select the template **pure HTML (4).**

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6418439-bOU0XkxiZhUH5ZJD12wY1oMuMEq4WdbGizTdwewgatPJJQHIhbytNteb4wcu19qD-0/image-20251126144753-4.png)
2. Click on the ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6418438-d05lKqzrUM43d7Zen6qC21JomoyuAZI5Cxy7oF48k1fT1wIjdexD4kqCKHB5ooyY-0/image-20251126144644-3.png) button and then **Make copy.**

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6418440-uGbgseheEZBAgDgZeKU8Glo4Jy50aHYewoDgZUlpx32Z4t7XCcUlgTs2bqm47mqR-0/image-20251126144952-5.png)
3. In the field **Name (1)**write a name for the template, then click on the **Source (2)**button. This will open an HTML editor, paste your template code there (3).

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6418474-MmOGUmkrQAfpXtlIw4u2LklP6Z6yWCby4lTy9ZGiXwJjGGifWIY6mfo7siK4QNwE-0/image-20251126145455-6.png)
4. When you are done, again click on the **Source (1)**buttonto check how your template looks visually and click **OK (2)** to save your template.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6418477-sBCgTEmpkjPOjYBiy0JAgSzcjSmXLBb9chq4o4liOQQHfHfRtrpZSGZPkMcfardG-0/image-20251126145728-7.png)

**Note!**

1. From SuperOffice 8.3 R03, you can import JSON format templates by clicking on the **Import** **(2)** button at the bottom of the **Message templates (1)**screen.

    ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6418501-LfXx4FO0nRYMe4XTWqLscjMyxmEN2D440Zsa7z0cHxzoJzGsj2Uk3k2hu6qKG0ea-0/image-20251126150638-8.png)
2. SuperOffice CRM offers more than 100 [templates for mailing](https://docs.superoffice.com/en/marketing/learn/message-templates.html) that you can download and use as a starting point or inspiration for your own mailing template that will reflect your company's branding.
