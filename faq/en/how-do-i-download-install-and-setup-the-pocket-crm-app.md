# How do I download, install and setup the Pocket CRM app?

Updated on 25 Feb 2026

I want to use SuperOffice on my phone. How do I download, install, and setup SuperOffice Pocket CRM application?

|  |
| --- |
| Pocket CRM can't log in to SuperOffice version 10 and later. You need to upgrade to [Mobile CRM](https://appstore.superoffice.com/superoffice-as/superoffice-mobile-crm) to benefit from the latest features. Please follow the instructions in [this article](https://docs.superoffice.com/en/mobile/superoffice-mobile/login.html) to start using it. |

Pocket CRM is included in the Onsite Subscription, and you can just download and install the Pocket CRM app. If you have an Onsite Buy license type, see the [deployment guide](https://docs.superoffice.com/en/mobile/pocket-crm/deploy.html).

To download, install, and configure your Pocket CRM, please follow the steps below:

1. Open a web browser on your mobile device and navigate to: [online.superoffice.com/mobile](https://online.superoffice.com/mobile)

2. Choose Google Play if you use an Android phone, or App Store if you use an Apple device.

3. Follow the process in the Play Store/App Store for downloading and installing the application.

4. Open the installed application on your mobile.
5. On the screen, you click **SuperOffice CRM**.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6555379-igudAS4M0Saa5X1sziHZSIRW9bET110pwnmFulPsDeYI2nMziTTr7kc2xQN4u3X8-0/image-20260225141114-1.png)

6. You will be asked to enter the URL from your NetServer installation.

   ![](https://online2.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/4038605-liFa6AW5mRgIrFSLXaSUdQkNrKLCE7g5fYdzEjIM97iSSUBWEE984pljwYJSKXTX-0/image.png)
7. To get it open, open your SuperOffice CRM on a browser, click on your **Personal settings** section, and choose **Pocket CRM settings**

   ![](https://online2.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/4394190-DH42BzT1T9X6iYO947f5EDCIJ2GPRSLwWMd2oGbaBzYaIltU9pZWNqQgdltAIKhr-0/image.png)
8. In the dialog, you’ll see the **Server URL for Pocket CRM.** Enter the following URL in the dialog you see on your mobile device, or click **OK**, and you’ll receive an e-mail. You can open the e-mail on your device and click on the link in the e-mail.

   ![](https://online2.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/4394191-g7OJR7NXMxkl627qJD2PCp6nw8fwS8XMNH1ZghOAZZNg4JRTa91cHFF9AyZDJcto-0/image.png)
9. Log in with the same e-mail address and password you use for the SuperOffice CRM, and you are ready to use SuperOffice Pocket CRM.
