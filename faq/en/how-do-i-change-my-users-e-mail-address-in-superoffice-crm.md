# How do I change my user’s e-mail address in SuperOffice CRM?

Updated on 24 Feb 2026

My last name has changed, therefore my e-mail address has also changed. How do I change it in SuperOffice CRM?

To change your e-mail address and last name of the SuperOffice user, you need to contact your SuperOffice administrator and ask them to follow the steps below:

1. Log in to [Settings and maintenance](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-access-administration-part-settings--maintenance-in-superoffice-crm/).
2. Click on **Users**(1) and in the list of users select your user (2).

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6553729-E9zRLRaPfoH2pT0rOEesQ4YK0sb8RmLlffxlkhwOa1U1f733ekOzEsTMRXXY62aN-0/image-20260224163102-2.png)
3. E-mail address has to be changed in both the **E-mail address** field (1) and the **User name** (2) field, then the **last name** (3) can be changed too, and the administrator has to **Save** (4) changes.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6553731-iROZiiN7ZjTcMh10yQ7Lsxrizaq0rFRZBg2U4b3wWHmeOOuJFDOE1kS0ls8gtgpb-0/image-20260224163313-3.png)
4. Now, when you log in to SuperOffice CRM, you need to use your new e-mail address as your username and the old password in the password field.

**Note!**

1. If your company uses Office 365 or Gmail as an identity provider, the changes to the e-mail address have to be made in your identity provider's site.
2. If you use the [Synchronizer](https://appstore.superoffice.com/infobridge-software-b-v-/synchronizer-for-superoffice) application together with SuperOffice Online, then after the change, the e-mail user needs to be re-linked in order to see the new email address. This needs to be done by the Synchronizer administrator user. But before that, the user who will be re-linked needs to clean his/her Outlook calendar. Please check [this article](https://infobridgekb.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/NS/pages/20906079/How+to+clean+the+Outlook+calendar?src=search) to see how it is done.

**For Onsite customers:**

1. User ID is not changed automatically. If it needs to be changed, please contact your SuperOffice administrator.
2. If your SuperOffice CRM uses Active Directory, last name and e-mail changes have to be made in your identity provider’s system.
