# How do I get an overview of all contacts without any follow-ups in SuperOffice CRM?

Updated on 22 May 2025

How do I get an overview of all contacts without any follow-ups (appointments, tasks, phone calls) in SuperOffice CRM?

This is the way you can make a list of contacts without follow-ups:

1. [Create a selection of all contacts with follow-ups](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-get-an-overview-of-all-contacts-without-any-follow-ups-in-superoffice-crm/#1)
2. [Create the interest “Have follow-ups¨ for contacts](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-get-an-overview-of-all-contacts-without-any-follow-ups-in-superoffice-crm/#2)
3. [Use a bulk update to update all relevant contacts](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-get-an-overview-of-all-contacts-without-any-follow-ups-in-superoffice-crm/#3)
4. [Create a contact selection that lists all contacts in SuperOffice CRM](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-get-an-overview-of-all-contacts-without-any-follow-ups-in-superoffice-crm/#4)
5. [Create a contact selection that lists only contacts with an interest](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-get-an-overview-of-all-contacts-without-any-follow-ups-in-superoffice-crm/#5)
6. [Create a combined selection that would list only the contacts without follow-ups](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-get-an-overview-of-all-contacts-without-any-follow-ups-in-superoffice-crm/#6)

You must have the [combined selection](https://docs.superoffice.com/en/search-options/selection/learn/combine.html) feature to get an overview of the contacts without assigned documents and/or without an e-mail.

**Note!** Only our Sales and Service Premium and Marketing plans have the combined selections feature available. Please contact your SuperOffice contact if you need this feature.

**Create a selection of all contacts with follow-ups**

To do so, please follow the steps below:

1. Click on the **Find** (1) button and then **Find Follow-ups** (2).

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6216167-D3ME7L9WJlT5RHPKQk3nhlrujpZZhkbhfJx3kGS2c7KdSY0jonso1hXLkOsSOwoW-0/image-20250522144224-1.png)
2. Next, in the **Criteria** (1) field click the arrow, which will unfold the list of the possible criteria for you to use. Choose **Type (1)**in the list box to the right of **Type**, click the arrow and select **Is one of** (2). In the last field on the line, select all the follow-up **types (3)** that you want to include in this search.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6216179-jYcn4crx2jISNrtXzrPDOgBFoDyZcBuI7Zkw0WZN2rdIpDCYpG7QiLMtW9ZIgaLi-0/image-20250522144538-2.png)
3. Then you can select the time period that should be considered in the selection. For example, **all follow-ups registered for the last 5 years including this year** (1). Click **Find** (2) to list all the follow-ups.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6216191-uixNOn7BBFc3U0aDjLYHMExSKjxYzOfbrePaOkeuRw1rBB8ONoO1LnjsPhjWUy91-0/image-20250522144755-4.png)
4. Once you have a list of documents click on the **Save as selection** button and [create a selection](https://docs.superoffice.com/en/search-options/selection/learn/create.html).
5. You have created a selection of follow-ups; however, it also has a Company/contact tab, also known as “Shadow selection” of the companies and contacts associated with these follow-ups. [Configure the columns](https://docs.superoffice.com/en/learn/section-tabs/configure-columns.html) in a way that you could see at least Company´s name Contact´s name and e-mail address. This will allow you to choose the correct contacts to select later.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6216516-SM9ASA0KWZZCjeUAtnzk4wJsGRyUgCiLQQHAm7wQzmdMmpalj4QowSGnJOxwqIJq-0/image-20250522161502-5.png)

**Create the interest “Have follow-ups¨ for contacts**

Please read [this article](https://docs.superoffice.com/en/admin/lists/learn/index.html) to learn how to create an interest for contacts. In this example, we will use a contact interest called **“Have follow-ups”.**

![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6216518-6FJLclg10je3BxXuaZdcwhyHAoCgZlDtZlxx1iBMCjbnMEyGTpl7DbA2WfADpEqP-0/image-20250522161705-6.png)

**Use a bulk update to update all relevant contacts**

To do so, please follow the steps below:

1. To select all the contacts, use the **Shift** keyboard for it. First click **Show All**, then select the first contact in the list and then scroll down until you reach the last and while keeping the **Shift key**pressed, click on it. Then, right-click on selected contacts and click on **Bulk update**. You can also click on **Task** button and select **Bulk update** from there too.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6216550-67yc8bBKOoEUg2RHO82fOkL8LUj0TlG1UQFI7TPbOAHn8easq7dhbomYjUxRqaTr-0/image-20250522162914-7.png)
2. Make sure you are in the **Contact (1)** tab, then select **Interest – add (2)** and choose the interest you created in the previous step and click **Update (3)**. It will select this interest on all the contacts you have selected previously.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6216564-YNSHe5Ie5uzhSqeJhWwMKjqkoa57DM1x7rRPNaozy92eYjuxLnnYSWHRGebsnHix-0/image-20250522163403-8.png)

**Create a contact selection that lists all contacts in SuperOffice CRM**

Please follow [this FAQ](https://community.superoffice.com/en/support-faqs/faq/how-do-i-get-a-list-of-all-my-companies-or-contacts-in-superoffice-crm/) to learn step by step how to create this selection.

**Create a contact selection that lists only contacts with the interest**

To do so, please follow the steps below:

1. Click on the **Find** (1) button and then **Find Contact**(2).
2. Next, choose **Contact interest** in the list box to the right of **Type**, click the arrow and select **Is equal to** the interest you created earlier (1). Click **Find** (2) and then follow the steps to [create a selection](http://docs.superoffice.com/en/search-options/selection/learn/create.html).

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6216584-cHEnh3Wtl0y1tBoxL4Ue2FzTlwlU94sAralQ5dOnfuqgyuRQgTT3Xy32FFkWTzcG-0/image-20250522164725-9.png)

**Create a combined selection that would list only the contacts without follow-ups**

To do so, please follow the steps below:

1. In one of the selections you created earlier, click on the **Task** (1) button and then **Combine selection with...** (2).

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6216585-0FUo1jbgSeSTRtmwzHXrsTR1NAhSxKb4WGWftyifOm9EBTEiUmfjJ72H9LI1Ozsj-0/image-20250522164954-10.png)
2. The new combine selection interface will be populated. Give it a meaningful name (1). As selection 1, choose **All contacts** (2), as selection 2, choose **All companies with with follow-ups** (3). As a combination type, select **Only in Selection 1** (4) option. Click on the **Save** (5) button to finish.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6216594-JdtMlQ9WXLAc3OIUZJ41AoWPbjug3O82wx2Old1kEsq0Aeyrnl2c6xZ0ugJE4e6q-0/image-20250522165438-11.png)
