# How do I start a remote support session with SuperOffice support on my phone?

Updated on 28 Dec 2025

How do I start a remote session with SuperOffice support on my phone?

To start a support remote session with SuperOffice Support, please follow the steps below:

1. Download the **TeamViewer QuickSupport** app, which allows a support agent to access your phone.
   - **For iOS devices:**

     Open the App Store, search for "*TeamViewer QuickSupport*", and click on the **Get** button to download the app.

     ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6465384-b5JQsbzZ9A1hJwxddPXkSxAqdc0EjZjxQsbSta5XpaYELX5zg47fxm83dDK1RLnB-0/image-20251228165131-1.png)

     **Note!** If you are reading this FAQ on your mobile, click [here](https://apps.apple.com/lt/app/teamviewer-quicksupport/id661649585) to download a *Teamviewer QuickSupport* app.
   - **For Android devices:**

     Open [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps), search for "*TeamViewer QuickSupport*", and click on the **Install** button to download the app.

     **Note!** If you are reading this FAQ on your mobile, click [here](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.teamviewer.quicksupport.market) to download a *Teamviewer QuickSupport* app.
2. Open the remote session initiation email sent by the support consultant on your phone, and click the remote session link.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6465385-uqQ36N2tZm60MlCF4lWG6KRr7iBdHphsiYWSwfjatouCp6W1cffSIEsmwNTx0QBW-0/image-20251228170022-2.png)

   **Note!** If you're unable to open the email on your phone, you can manually enter the remote session link in your phone’s internet browser.
3. The link will open the TeamViewer application on your phone. Please wait while the support agent initiates the connection.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6465387-wLh0R28S01yAHZobwEzOcLMJPIHMpEKkEK90OwUIqfcuroPzRsbmyjKJDyMJjxAq-0/image-20251228170342-4.png)
4. When the support agent starts the session, you will be prompted to allow access. Click **Allow Access** to give the agent permission to access your phone.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6465386-p4hS9yJGyNkpczewB0FPAgBZ0fiQAD2CPCGmzsTiD0NOqsHlHkut49SiarUYynzw-0/image-20251228170258-3.png)
5. Finally, click **Start Broadcast** to begin sharing your screen with the support agent.

   ![](https://online3.superoffice.com/Cust1990/CS/scripts/customer.fcgi/getAttachment/6465388-6DxtIpPo2mo3Era9E0jKXUxaswlBYuAoFwnfpULmU9LBXjR4TjmdacIwiLF8B7ib-0/image-20251228170429-5.png)
